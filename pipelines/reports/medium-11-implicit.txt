FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario medium-11)

Our team has one car available, able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Naukunda shelter [naukunda-shelter].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Naukunda shelter [naukunda-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Syaphru school [syaphru-school] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.

ROAD CONDITIONS
- The Galchhi care home [galchhi-care-home] to Gosaikunda care home [gosaikunda-care-home] road is muddy but passable, about 55 minutes by car.
- The drive from Dupcheshwar market [dupcheshwar-market] to Shivapuri market [shivapuri-market] takes about 24 minutes.
- Drivers report the road between Gosaikunda care home [gosaikunda-care-home] and Panchakanya temple [panchakanya-temple] is clear; it takes roughly 34 minutes.
- It is a 16-minute drive between Naukunda shelter [naukunda-shelter] and Shivapuri market [shivapuri-market], with some debris but no blockages.
- A landslide has buried the road between Naukunda shelter [naukunda-shelter] and Dupcheshwar market [dupcheshwar-market] (normally a 7-minute drive).
- Drivers report the road between Naukunda shelter [naukunda-shelter] and Gosaikunda care home [gosaikunda-care-home] is clear; it takes roughly 20 minutes.
- A landslide has buried the road between Gosaikunda care home [gosaikunda-care-home] and Shivapuri market [shivapuri-market] (normally a 13-minute drive).
- Police have closed the Dupcheshwar market [dupcheshwar-market] to Syaphru school [syaphru-school] road after part of it collapsed into the river. It is normally 21 minutes by car.
- It is a 8-minute drive between Benighat market [benighat-market] and Gosaikunda care home [gosaikunda-care-home], with some debris but no blockages.
Any road not listed here does not exist or cannot be used.
