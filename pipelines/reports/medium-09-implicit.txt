FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario medium-09)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1], [car2] at Thakre shelter [thakre-shelter].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Thakre shelter [thakre-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Kakani school [kakani-school] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
