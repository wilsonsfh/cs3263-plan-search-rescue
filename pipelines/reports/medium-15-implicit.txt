FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario medium-15)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Jwalamukhi shelter [jwalamukhi-shelter]; [car2] at Gosaikunda hospital [gosaikunda-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Volunteers at Jwalamukhi shelter [jwalamukhi-shelter] say they have room for more families.
Gosaikunda hospital [gosaikunda-hospital] is on high ground and its staff are receiving evacuees.
