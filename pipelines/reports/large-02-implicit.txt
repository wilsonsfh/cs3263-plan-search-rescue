FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario large-02)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Bidur shelter [bidur-shelter]; [car2] at Suryagadhi hospital [suryagadhi-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Bidur shelter [bidur-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Suryagadhi hospital [suryagadhi-hospital] is accepting people brought in from the flooded areas.
Tarkeshwar school [tarkeshwar-school] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
