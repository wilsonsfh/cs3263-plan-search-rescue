FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario medium-01)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1], [car2] at Kakani shelter [kakani-shelter].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Volunteers at Kakani shelter [kakani-shelter] say they have room for more families.
Tarkeshwar school [tarkeshwar-school] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.

ROAD CONDITIONS
- It is a 38-minute drive between Benighat farm [benighat-farm] and Uttargaya farm [uttargaya-farm], with some debris but no blockages.
- The bridge on the Benighat farm [benighat-farm] to Myagang market [myagang-market] road (54 minutes in normal conditions) was washed away last night.
- The drive from Tarkeshwar school [tarkeshwar-school] to Naukunda school [naukunda-school] takes about 17 minutes.
- The Myagang market [myagang-market] to Naukunda school [naukunda-school] road is muddy but passable, about 29 minutes by car.
- The Tarkeshwar school [tarkeshwar-school] to Benighat farm [benighat-farm] road is muddy but passable, about 14 minutes by car.
- Drivers report the road between Kakani shelter [kakani-shelter] and Naukunda school [naukunda-school] is clear; it takes roughly 25 minutes.
- Police have closed the Uttargaya farm [uttargaya-farm] to Belkot farm [belkot-farm] road after part of it collapsed into the river. It is normally 11 minutes by car.
- The bridge on the Tarkeshwar school [tarkeshwar-school] to Tadi school [tadi-school] road (7 minutes in normal conditions) was washed away last night.
- The Gosaikunda temple [gosaikunda-temple] to Uttargaya farm [uttargaya-farm] road is muddy but passable, about 5 minutes by car.
- The Benighat farm [benighat-farm] to Tadi school [tadi-school] road is muddy but passable, about 21 minutes by car.
- The Kakani shelter [kakani-shelter] to Tarkeshwar school [tarkeshwar-school] road is muddy but passable, about 12 minutes by car.
Any road not listed here does not exist or cannot be used.
