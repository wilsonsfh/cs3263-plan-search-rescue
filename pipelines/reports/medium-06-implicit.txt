FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario medium-06)

Our team has one car available, able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Jwalamukhi shelter [jwalamukhi-shelter].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Jwalamukhi shelter [jwalamukhi-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Gajuri school [gajuri-school] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.

ROAD CONDITIONS
- The drive from Jwalamukhi shelter [jwalamukhi-shelter] to Belkot farm [belkot-farm] takes about 20 minutes.
- The Gajuri school [gajuri-school] to Kalika temple [kalika-temple] road is muddy but passable, about 19 minutes by car.
- Police have closed the Jwalamukhi shelter [jwalamukhi-shelter] to Syaphru care home [syaphru-care-home] road after part of it collapsed into the river. It is normally 56 minutes by car.
- The drive from Syaphru care home [syaphru-care-home] to Kalika temple [kalika-temple] takes about 30 minutes.
- A landslide has buried the road between Belkot farm [belkot-farm] and Kalika temple [kalika-temple] (normally a 23-minute drive).
- Drivers report the road between Gajuri school [gajuri-school] and Belkot farm [belkot-farm] is clear; it takes roughly 19 minutes.
- Floodwater is running fast over the Kalika temple [kalika-temple] to Nilkantha temple [nilkantha-temple] road; a truck was swept off it this morning. The drive usually takes 12 minutes.
- It is a 5-minute drive between Suryagadhi market [suryagadhi-market] and Nilkantha temple [nilkantha-temple], with some debris but no blockages.
- The Jwalamukhi shelter [jwalamukhi-shelter] to Gajuri school [gajuri-school] road is muddy but passable, about 19 minutes by car.
Any road not listed here does not exist or cannot be used.

STRANDED CIVILIANS
- At Kalika temple [kalika-temple], residents report six frail senior citizens waiting for help.
- At Nilkantha temple [nilkantha-temple], residents report an elderly widow waiting for help.
- At Suryagadhi market [suryagadhi-market], residents report a bus driver waiting for help.
- At Syaphru care home [syaphru-care-home], residents report a farmer with a badly cut arm waiting for help.
