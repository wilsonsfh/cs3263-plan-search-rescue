FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario medium-12)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Panchakanya shelter [panchakanya-shelter]; [car2] at Shivapuri hospital [shivapuri-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Panchakanya shelter [panchakanya-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Shivapuri hospital [shivapuri-hospital] is on high ground and its staff are receiving evacuees.
Trishuli temple [trishuli-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
