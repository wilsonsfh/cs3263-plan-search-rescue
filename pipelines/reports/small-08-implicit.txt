FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario small-08)

Our team has one car available, able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Jwalamukhi shelter [jwalamukhi-shelter].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Volunteers at Jwalamukhi shelter [jwalamukhi-shelter] say they have room for more families.
Dupcheshwar temple [dupcheshwar-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
