FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario large-05)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Thakre shelter [thakre-shelter]; [car2] at Nilkantha hospital [nilkantha-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Thakre shelter [thakre-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Nilkantha hospital [nilkantha-hospital] is accepting people brought in from the flooded areas.
Naukunda temple [naukunda-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.

ROAD CONDITIONS
- Police have closed the Trishuli care home [trishuli-care-home] to Likhu temple [likhu-temple] road after part of it collapsed into the river. It is normally 13 minutes by car.
- The Naukunda temple [naukunda-temple] to Likhu temple [likhu-temple] road is muddy but passable, about 17 minutes by car.
- It is a 20-minute drive between Likhu temple [likhu-temple] and Gajuri care home [gajuri-care-home], with some debris but no blockages.
- It is a 29-minute drive between Likhu temple [likhu-temple] and Kispang farm [kispang-farm], with some debris but no blockages.
- It is a 21-minute drive between Belkot village [belkot-village] and Tadi village [tadi-village], with some debris but no blockages.
- Floodwater is running fast over the Thakre shelter [thakre-shelter] to Trishuli care home [trishuli-care-home] road; a truck was swept off it this morning. The drive usually takes 23 minutes.
