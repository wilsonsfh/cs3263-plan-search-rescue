FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario small-02)

Our team has one car available, able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Shivapuri shelter [shivapuri-shelter].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Shivapuri shelter [shivapuri-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Kispang temple [kispang-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.

ROAD CONDITIONS
- Drivers report the road between Kispang temple [kispang-temple] and Belkot market [belkot-market] is clear; it takes roughly 6 minutes.
- It is a 9-minute drive between Trishuli farm [trishuli-farm] and Kispang temple [kispang-temple], with some debris but no blockages.
- Drivers report the road between Shivapuri shelter [shivapuri-shelter] and Trishuli farm [trishuli-farm] is clear; it takes roughly 39 minutes.
- It is a 45-minute drive between Shivapuri shelter [shivapuri-shelter] and Kispang temple [kispang-temple], with some debris but no blockages.
- It is a 16-minute drive between Trishuli farm [trishuli-farm] and Belkot market [belkot-market], with some debris but no blockages.
- Drivers report the road between Shivapuri shelter [shivapuri-shelter] and Kalika village [kalika-village] is clear; it takes roughly 7 minutes.
Any road not listed here does not exist or cannot be used.

STRANDED CIVILIANS
