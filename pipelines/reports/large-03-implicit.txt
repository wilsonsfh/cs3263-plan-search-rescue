FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario large-03)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Benighat shelter [benighat-shelter]; [car2] at Gajuri hospital [gajuri-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Volunteers at Benighat shelter [benighat-shelter] say they have room for more families.
Gajuri hospital [gajuri-hospital] is on high ground and its staff are receiving evacuees.
Syaphru school [syaphru-school] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
