FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario small-03)

Our team has one car available, able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Myagang shelter [myagang-shelter].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Volunteers at Myagang shelter [myagang-shelter] say they have room for more families.
Benighat temple [benighat-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.

- The Myagang shelter [myagang-shelter] to Nilkantha market [nilkantha-market] road is muddy but passable, about 18 minutes by car.
- The Jwalamukhi village [jwalamukhi-village] to Syaphru school [syaphru-school] road is muddy but passable, about 10 minutes by car.
- It is a 27-minute drive between Myagang shelter [myagang-shelter] and Bidur village [bidur-village], with some debris but no blockages.
