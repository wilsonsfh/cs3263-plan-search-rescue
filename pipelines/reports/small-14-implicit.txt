FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario small-14)

Our team has one car available, able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Tarkeshwar shelter [tarkeshwar-shelter].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Volunteers at Tarkeshwar shelter [tarkeshwar-shelter] say they have room for more families.
Myagang temple [myagang-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.

ROAD CONDITIONS
- Police have closed the Shivapuri farm [shivapuri-farm] to Gosaikunda village [gosaikunda-village] road after part of it collapsed into the river. It is normally 26 minutes by car.
- Floodwater is running fast over the Shivapuri farm [shivapuri-farm] to Benighat farm [benighat-farm] road; a truck was swept off it this morning. The drive usually takes 24 minutes.
- The drive from Shivapuri farm [shivapuri-farm] to Myagang temple [myagang-temple] takes about 19 minutes.
- The Tarkeshwar shelter [tarkeshwar-shelter] to Myagang temple [myagang-temple] road is muddy but passable, about 20 minutes by car.
- Drivers report the road between Tadi farm [tadi-farm] and Myagang temple [myagang-temple] is clear; it takes roughly 22 minutes.
- Drivers report the road between Benighat farm [benighat-farm] and Myagang temple [myagang-temple] is clear; it takes roughly 21 minutes.
- The drive from Gosaikunda village [gosaikunda-village] to Tadi farm [tadi-farm] takes about 58 minutes.
Any road not listed here does not exist or cannot be used.

STRANDED CIVILIANS
- At Myagang temple [myagang-temple], residents report three frail senior citizens and three shopkeepers waiting for help.
- At Tadi farm [tadi-farm], residents report two men and women from the cooperative waiting for help.
Families have said they are willing to travel separately if needed.
