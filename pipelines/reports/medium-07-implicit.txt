FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario medium-07)

Our team has one car available, able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Kakani shelter [kakani-shelter].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Kakani shelter [kakani-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Myagang hospital [myagang-hospital] is on high ground and its staff are receiving evacuees.
Panchakanya school [panchakanya-school] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
