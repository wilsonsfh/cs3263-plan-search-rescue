FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario medium-14)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Dupcheshwar shelter [dupcheshwar-shelter]; [car2] at Panchakanya hospital [panchakanya-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Volunteers at Dupcheshwar shelter [dupcheshwar-shelter] say they have room for more families.
Panchakanya hospital [panchakanya-hospital] is accepting people brought in from the flooded areas.
Tadi school [tadi-school] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
