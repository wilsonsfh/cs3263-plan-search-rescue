FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario medium-13)

Our team has one car available, able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Tarkeshwar shelter [tarkeshwar-shelter].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Volunteers at Tarkeshwar shelter [tarkeshwar-shelter] say they have room for more families.
Panchakanya temple [panchakanya-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.

ROAD CONDITIONS
- It is a 11-minute drive between Tarkeshwar shelter [tarkeshwar-shelter] and Dhunche village [dhunche-village], with some debris but no blockages.
- It is a 21-minute drive between Kalika farm [kalika-farm] and Gajuri farm [gajuri-farm], with some debris but no blockages.
- The Panchakanya temple [panchakanya-temple] to Gajuri farm [gajuri-farm] road is muddy but passable, about 16 minutes by car.
- A landslide has buried the road between Kalika farm [kalika-farm] and Panchakanya temple [panchakanya-temple] (normally a 7-minute drive).
- Drivers report the road between Trishuli care home [trishuli-care-home] and Gajuri farm [gajuri-farm] is clear; it takes roughly 5 minutes.
- Drivers report the road between Dhunche village [dhunche-village] and Gajuri farm [gajuri-farm] is clear; it takes roughly 21 minutes.
- Floodwater is running fast over the Trishuli care home [trishuli-care-home] to Nilkantha temple [nilkantha-temple] road; a truck was swept off it this morning. The drive usually takes 7 minutes.
- A landslide has buried the road between Kalika farm [kalika-farm] and Dhunche village [dhunche-village] (normally a 40-minute drive).
- The Kalika farm [kalika-farm] to Gosaikunda care home [gosaikunda-care-home] road is muddy but passable, about 27 minutes by car.
- The Kalika farm [kalika-farm] to Nilkantha temple [nilkantha-temple] road is muddy but passable, about 20 minutes by car.
Any road not listed here does not exist or cannot be used.

STRANDED CIVILIANS
- At Dhunche village [dhunche-village], residents report a farmer with a badly cut arm and two pupils waiting for help.
- At Gajuri farm [gajuri-farm], residents report five working-age adults waiting for help.
- At Gosaikunda care home [gosaikunda-care-home], residents report three men and women from the cooperative waiting for help.
Families have said they are willing to travel separately if needed.
