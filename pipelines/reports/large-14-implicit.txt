FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario large-14)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Trishuli shelter [trishuli-shelter]; [car2] at Dhunche hospital [dhunche-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Trishuli shelter [trishuli-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Dhunche hospital [dhunche-hospital] is on high ground and its staff are receiving evacuees.
Bidur temple [bidur-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
