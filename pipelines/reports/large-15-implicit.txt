FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario large-15)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Suryagadhi shelter [suryagadhi-shelter]; [car2] at Kalika hospital [kalika-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Suryagadhi shelter [suryagadhi-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Kalika hospital [kalika-hospital] is on high ground and its staff are receiving evacuees.
Tadi temple [tadi-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
