FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario small-11)

Our team has one car available, able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Myagang shelter [myagang-shelter].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Myagang shelter [myagang-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Gosaikunda temple [gosaikunda-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.

ROAD CONDITIONS
- It is a 45-minute drive between Dhunche farm [dhunche-farm] and Galchhi village [galchhi-village], with some debris but no blockages.
- The Myagang shelter [myagang-shelter] to Gosaikunda temple [gosaikunda-temple] road is muddy but passable, about 51 minutes by car.
- Floodwater is running fast over the Dhunche farm [dhunche-farm] to Gosaikunda temple [gosaikunda-temple] road; a truck was swept off it this morning. The drive usually takes 19 minutes.
- It is a 26-minute drive between Myagang shelter [myagang-shelter] and Suryagadhi market [suryagadhi-market], with some debris but no blockages.
- It is a 25-minute drive between Suryagadhi market [suryagadhi-market] and Galchhi village [galchhi-village], with some debris but no blockages.
- The Myagang shelter [myagang-shelter] to Galchhi village [galchhi-village] road is muddy but passable, about 14 minutes by car.
Any road not listed here does not exist or cannot be used.

STRANDED CIVILIANS
- At Gosaikunda temple [gosaikunda-temple], residents report a farmer and an elderly couple waiting for help.
- At Suryagadhi market [suryagadhi-market], residents report three people hurt by falling debris waiting for help.
Families have said they are willing to travel separately if needed.
