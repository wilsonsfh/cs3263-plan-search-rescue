FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario medium-03)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Uttargaya shelter [uttargaya-shelter]; [car2] at Thakre hospital [thakre-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Uttargaya shelter [uttargaya-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Thakre hospital [thakre-hospital] is on high ground and its staff are receiving evacuees.
Kalika temple [kalika-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
