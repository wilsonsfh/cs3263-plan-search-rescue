FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario small-09)

Our team has one car available, able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Kispang shelter [kispang-shelter].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Kispang shelter [kispang-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Galchhi school [galchhi-school] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.

ROAD CONDITIONS
- The Nilkantha village [nilkantha-village] to Thakre temple [thakre-temple] road is muddy but passable, about 26 minutes by car.
- The drive from Thakre temple [thakre-temple] to Kalika market [kalika-market] takes about 14 minutes.
- Floodwater is running fast over the Kispang shelter [kispang-shelter] to Nilkantha village [nilkantha-village] road; a truck was swept off it this morning. The drive usually takes 9 minutes.
- Drivers report the road between Galchhi school [galchhi-school] and Kalika market [kalika-market] is clear; it takes roughly 21 minutes.
- The Galchhi school [galchhi-school] to Nilkantha village [nilkantha-village] road is muddy but passable, about 9 minutes by car.
- It is a 37-minute drive between Thakre temple [thakre-temple] and Tadi village [tadi-village], with some debris but no blockages.
- Drivers report the road between Galchhi school [galchhi-school] and Thakre temple [thakre-temple] is clear; it takes roughly 27 minutes.
Any road not listed here does not exist or cannot be used.

STRANDED CIVILIANS
- At Kalika market [kalika-market], residents report three kids from the nursery class waiting for help.
- At Galchhi school [galchhi-school], residents report four kids from the nursery class waiting for help.
Families have said they are willing to travel separately if needed.
