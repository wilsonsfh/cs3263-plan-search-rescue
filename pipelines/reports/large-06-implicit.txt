FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario large-06)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Kispang shelter [kispang-shelter]; [car2] at Benighat hospital [benighat-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Kispang shelter [kispang-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Benighat hospital [benighat-hospital] is accepting people brought in from the flooded areas.
Dhunche temple [dhunche-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
