FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario large-11)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Tarkeshwar shelter [tarkeshwar-shelter]; [car2] at Dupcheshwar hospital [dupcheshwar-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Tarkeshwar shelter [tarkeshwar-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Dupcheshwar hospital [dupcheshwar-hospital] is accepting people brought in from the flooded areas.
Myagang temple [myagang-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
