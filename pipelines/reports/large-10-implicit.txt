FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario large-10)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Panchakanya shelter [panchakanya-shelter]; [car2] at Tadi hospital [tadi-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Volunteers at Panchakanya shelter [panchakanya-shelter] say they have room for more families.
Tadi hospital [tadi-hospital] is accepting people brought in from the flooded areas.
Dhunche temple [dhunche-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
