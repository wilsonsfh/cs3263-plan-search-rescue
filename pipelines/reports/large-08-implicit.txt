FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario large-08)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Kakani shelter [kakani-shelter]; [car2] at Kalika hospital [kalika-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Volunteers at Kakani shelter [kakani-shelter] say they have room for more families.
Kalika hospital [kalika-hospital] is accepting people brought in from the flooded areas.
Naukunda school [naukunda-school] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
