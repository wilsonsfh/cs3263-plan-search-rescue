FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario medium-02)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Benighat shelter [benighat-shelter]; [car2] at Dhunche hospital [dhunche-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Benighat shelter [benighat-shelter] is above the flood line and is taking in evacuees.
Dhunche hospital [dhunche-hospital] is accepting people brought in from the flooded areas.
Kalika temple [kalika-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.

ROAD CONDITIONS
- Drivers report the road between Likhu village [likhu-village] and Kalika temple [kalika-temple] is clear; it takes roughly 11 minutes.
- Drivers report the road between Benighat shelter [benighat-shelter] and Jwalamukhi care home [jwalamukhi-care-home] is clear; it takes roughly 21 minutes.
- The drive from Dhunche hospital [dhunche-hospital] to Uttargaya care home [uttargaya-care-home] takes about 12 minutes.
- It is a 30-minute drive between Dhunche hospital [dhunche-hospital] and Kalika temple [kalika-temple], with some debris but no blockages.
- The drive from Jwalamukhi care home [jwalamukhi-care-home] to Kalika temple [kalika-temple] takes about 19 minutes.
- The Dhunche hospital [dhunche-hospital] to Likhu village [likhu-village] road is muddy but passable, about 22 minutes by car.
- A landslide has buried the road between Benighat shelter [benighat-shelter] and Kalika temple [kalika-temple] (normally a 8-minute drive).
- Drivers report the road between Jwalamukhi care home [jwalamukhi-care-home] and Nilkantha farm [nilkantha-farm] is clear; it takes roughly 19 minutes.
- Drivers report the road between Benighat shelter [benighat-shelter] and Nilkantha farm [nilkantha-farm] is clear; it takes roughly 22 minutes.
Any road not listed here does not exist or cannot be used.

STRANDED CIVILIANS
- At Likhu village [likhu-village], residents report four men and women from the cooperative and four people hurt by falling debris waiting for help.
- At Nilkantha farm [nilkantha-farm], residents report a young woman in her twenties and two residents with fractures and cuts waiting for help.
- At Kalika temple [kalika-temple], residents report two pensioners waiting for help.
Families have said they are willing to travel separately if needed.
