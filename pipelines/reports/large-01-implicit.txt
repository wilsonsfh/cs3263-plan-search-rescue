FIELD REPORT - Nuwakot district flood response (scenario large-01)

Our team has two cars available, each able to carry four passengers. Currently: [car1] at Myagang shelter [myagang-shelter]; [car2] at Uttargaya hospital [uttargaya-hospital].

SAFE LOCATIONS
Volunteers at Myagang shelter [myagang-shelter] say they have room for more families.
Uttargaya hospital [uttargaya-hospital] is on high ground and its staff are receiving evacuees.
Tadi temple [tadi-temple] sits on higher ground, but it has no food, water or medical staff and the authorities have not designated it as a place to bring people.
