FIELD DISPATCHES (several sources; listed in the order they reached the control room)
[09:30] District Police Office (situation report): Residents at the temple in Kaule say three injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris, an elderly woman with a broken hip and a child with a deep gash on his leg), one child and two uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Our team counted one senior citizen and four able-bodied adults at Bidur, all needing evacuation. Hillside collapse: the Bageshwari–Tadi road disappeared under a landslide overnight. The Kaule–Lachyang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The Ramche–Suryagadhi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 15 minutes to the usual journey. At the temple in Suryagadhi, two schoolchildren, an old man and six able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. A government helicopter was seen flying north this morning but did not land.
