FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
At Narjamandap, four children and one able-bodied adult are waiting to be rescued. Residents at the temple in Kaule say three injured people (a teenage girl with a fractured arm, an old man with a head wound and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), one child and 2 other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Boulders and fallen trees make the Kaule–Taruka road impassable for any vehicle. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Laharepauwa–Suryagadhi road; it is open again at normal speed. The Laharepauwa–Ramche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Residents at the temple in Suryagadhi say two children, one senior citizen and six other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at the school in Kispang: one injured person (an old man with a head wound), three schoolchildren, two senior citizens and one able-bodied adult. Floodwater has turned the road from Haku to Laharepauwa into a river. Hillside collapse: the Bageshwari–Tadi road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Floodwater has turned the road from Kispang to Taruka into a river. The Bidur–Salme road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. At Taruka, three children, one senior citizen and four uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Residents at the temple in Tadi say 2 schoolchildren and four able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at the school in Bageshwari: three schoolchildren, an old man and two able-bodied adults. The Ramche–Suryagadhi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 15 minutes to the usual journey. A landslide has buried the road between Kispang and Lachyang under metres of mud. At Laharepauwa, 2 injured people (a man with a crushed foot and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), an elderly couple and 4 uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Stranded at the school in Shikharbesi: 7 schoolchildren, three elderly residents and one other adult. The Narjamandap–Suryagadhi road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. At Lachyang, three schoolchildren, three senior citizens and six uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The Kaule–Lachyang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. At Ramche, one adult is waiting to be rescued. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Narjamandap–Shikharbesi road; it is open again at normal speed. Our team counted an old man and 4 able-bodied adults at Bidur, all needing evacuation. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
