FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The Bhalche–Kumari road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The suspension bridge on the Ghyangphedi–Kumari route was swept away by the river. A landslide has buried the road between Belkot and Kalikasthan under metres of mud. The Dupcheshwar–Madanpur road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Residents at the temple in Tupche say two injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris and a child with a deep gash on his leg), two elderly residents and five able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Bhalche–Dupcheshwar road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Boulders and fallen trees make the Dandagaun–Thaprek road impassable for any vehicle. The Dupcheshwar–Panchakanya road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Our team counted two injured people (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle and an elderly woman with a broken hip), four schoolchildren and six uninjured adults at the health post in Thansing, all needing evacuation. The Dandagaun–Tupche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Residents at Ramche say two injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip and an old man with a head wound), four schoolchildren, one elderly resident and five uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at Panchakanya: one schoolchild, an elderly couple and five able-bodied adults. Stranded at the ward office in Bhalche: one injured person (a teenage girl with a fractured arm), three schoolchildren and two other adults. Stranded at Belkot: one child and 2 able-bodied adults. Our team counted 3 injured people (a woman with burns on her arms, an old man with a head wound and a child with a deep gash on his leg) and 6 uninjured adults at Madanpur, all needing evacuation. Residents at the temple in Rautbesi say one injured person (a child with a deep gash on his leg), 4 schoolchildren, two senior citizens and 2 able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Kaule–Thaprek road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds a quarter of an hour to the usual journey. The Kumari–Tupche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds a quarter of an hour to the usual journey. The Bhalche–Ramche road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Everyone from Dupcheshwar has already been airlifted out. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Laharepauwa–Ramche road; it is open again at normal speed. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Narjamandap–Rautbesi road; it is open again at normal speed. Kumari was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. The Battar–Chhap road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Stranded at Dandagaun: two children and 2 uninjured adults. The Bhalche–Madanpur road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Floodwater has turned the road from Betrawati to Laharepauwa into a river. At Lachyang, four children and three uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The only bridge linking Battar and Thansing has collapsed. Our team counted one injured person (a woman with burns on her arms), 3 children and half a dozen other adults at the health post in Battar, all needing evacuation. The Bhalche–Lachyang road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Our team counted three schoolchildren, two senior citizens and 6 uninjured adults at the ward office in Kalikasthan, all needing evacuation. The Dandagaun–Kumari road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Our team counted one injured person (a man with a crushed foot), 4 children, 3 elderly residents and six able-bodied adults at the health post in Betrawati, all needing evacuation. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Dupcheshwar–Kaule road; it is open again at normal speed. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Thansing–Tupche road; it is open again at normal speed. At the school in Thaprek, four children and two other adults are waiting to be rescued. The Belkot–Laharepauwa road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. At Laharepauwa, 3 injured people (a woman with burns on her arms, a teenage girl with a fractured arm and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), four schoolchildren, an elderly couple and two able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Our team counted 2 senior citizens and four able-bodied adults at Narjamandap, all needing evacuation. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
