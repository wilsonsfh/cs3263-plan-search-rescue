[13:15] Sunita Lama, The Himalayan Times (on the ground): Confirmed headcount: Residents at Dhaibung say four schoolchildren, an old man and five other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted four schoolchildren and three other adults at Madanpur, all needing evacuation. The Jiling–Rautbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds a quarter of an hour to the usual journey. Police have checked the Belkot–Narjamandap road: the social media reports were false and the road is open as normal. Everyone from Chaughada has already been airlifted out. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted two injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris and a teenage girl with a fractured arm) and six able-bodied adults at the ward office in Rautbesi, all needing evacuation. Confirmed headcount: At Goljung, three injured people (a woman with burns on her arms, an elderly woman with a broken hip and a teenage girl with a fractured arm), four children and one able-bodied adult are waiting to be rescued. The only bridge linking Jiling and Taruka has collapsed. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted 4 adults at Narjamandap, all needing evacuation. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted one child and four uninjured adults at the temple in Ghyangphedi, all needing evacuation. Police have checked the Okharpauwa–Rautbesi road: the social media reports were false and the road is open as normal. Everyone from Okharpauwa has already been airlifted out. Stranded at Thansing: three children, one elderly resident and two uninjured adults. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted 2 schoolchildren, two senior citizens and one uninjured adult at the temple in Likhu, all needing evacuation. Police have checked the Ghyangphedi–Taruka road: the social media reports were false and the road is open as normal. A landslide has buried the road between Goljung and Salme under metres of mud. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted three senior citizens and three other adults at Taruka, all needing evacuation. Floodwater has turned the road from Chaughada to Dhaibung into a river, cutting a road that was open earlier today. A government helicopter was seen flying north this morning but did not land. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted two injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip and a man with a crushed foot) and 5 able-bodied adults at the ward office in Salme, all needing evacuation.
