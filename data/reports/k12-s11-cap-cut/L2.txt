FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Residents at Goljung say three injured people (a child with a deep gash on his leg, an old man with a head wound and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), 4 schoolchildren and 1 uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At Dhaibung, 4 children, one elderly resident and 5 able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. The Chaughada–Dhaibung road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. The suspension bridge on the Jiling–Taruka route was swept away by the river. The Kaule–Shikharbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The Ghyangphedi–Taruka road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. Locals warned us about the Okharpauwa–Rautbesi road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. At the school in Jiling, one injured person (a woman with burns on her arms), nine children, one senior citizen and one other adult are waiting to be rescued. Residents at Narjamandap say 4 adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Our team counted three schoolchildren, an old man and 2 uninjured adults at Thansing, all needing evacuation. Locals warned us about the Belkot–Narjamandap road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. The Okharpauwa–Taruka road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. Stranded at the temple in Likhu: two children, two senior citizens and 1 able-bodied adult. Stranded at the ward office in Salme: two injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris and a woman with burns on her arms) and five able-bodied adults. The Narjamandap–Shikharbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Everyone from Chaughada has already been airlifted out. Residents at the ward office in Rautbesi say two injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle) and six other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at Shikharbesi: three senior citizens and one uninjured adult. The only bridge linking Belkot and Salme has collapsed. A landslide has buried the road between Goljung and Salme under metres of mud. Everyone from Okharpauwa has already been airlifted out. The Jiling–Rautbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 15 minutes to the usual journey. The suspension bridge on the Likhu–Taruka route was swept away by the river. Stranded at the temple in Ghyangphedi: one schoolchild and four uninjured adults. At Madanpur, four schoolchildren and three uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Residents at Taruka say three senior citizens and three other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Chaughada–Thansing road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
