FIELD DISPATCHES (several sources; listed in the order they reached the control room)
[09:30] District Police Office (situation report): At the temple in Gerkhu, one adult is waiting to be rescued. The only bridge linking Meghang and Panchakanya has collapsed. In Kathmandu, schools have been closed for three days as a precaution. Residents at the school in Meghang say nine children, one elderly resident and one other adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors.
[13:15] Sunita Lama, The Himalayan Times (on the ground): Our team counted three injured people (a teenage girl with a fractured arm, an elderly woman with a broken hip and a child with a deep gash on his leg), 6 schoolchildren, one elderly resident and two uninjured adults at the school in Battar, all needing evacuation. Confirmed headcount: At the health post in Madanpur, one injured person (a child with a deep gash on his leg), two schoolchildren and one able-bodied adult are waiting to be rescued. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Madanpur–Panchakanya road; it is open again at normal speed. Confirmed headcount: Stranded at the temple in Samundratar: one injured person (a man with a crushed foot), three children and 5 uninjured adults. The Gerkhu–Samundratar road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Laharepauwa–Panchakanya road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Stranded at the health post in Laharepauwa: three senior citizens and three able-bodied adults. Confirmed headcount: At the health post in Bhalche, three injured people (a man with a crushed foot, a woman with burns on her arms and a teenage girl with a fractured arm), one schoolchild, two elderly residents and six able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Boulders and fallen trees make the Kakani–Laharepauwa road impassable for any vehicle. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Bhalche–Meghang road; it is open again at normal speed. Our team counted a baby and 3 uninjured adults at Kakani, all needing evacuation. A government helicopter was seen flying north this morning but did not land. Debris has completely choked the road between Betrawati and Samundratar, cutting a road that was open earlier today. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Gerkhu–Kakani road; it is open again at normal speed. The Madanpur–Meghang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds a quarter of an hour to the usual journey.
[06:45] Radio Nuwakot FM (local radio, early bulletin): The Betrawati–Samundratar road is still passable this morning, drivers say. Around 1 people are believed to be stranded in Madanpur (unconfirmed estimate). Mobile networks remain patchy across the district. Around 11 people are believed to be stranded in Samundratar (unconfirmed estimate). Debris is blocking the Madanpur–Panchakanya road. Around 16 people are believed to be stranded in Bhalche (unconfirmed estimate). Debris is blocking the Gerkhu–Kakani road. Debris is blocking the Bhalche–Meghang road.
Anything not mentioned in these dispatches is in its usual condition, and no one else is known to be stranded.
