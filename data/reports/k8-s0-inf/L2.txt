FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Boulders and fallen trees make the Kakani–Laharepauwa road impassable for any vehicle. Residents at the temple in Samundratar say one injured person (a man with a crushed foot), three schoolchildren and five uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Gerkhu–Kakani road; it is open again at normal speed. The suspension bridge on the Meghang–Panchakanya route was swept away by the river. At the health post in Laharepauwa, three senior citizens and three other adults are waiting to be rescued. Stranded at the school in Meghang: nine children, one elderly resident and 1 other adult. Stranded at the health post in Madanpur: one injured person (a teenage girl with a fractured arm), two schoolchildren and one uninjured adult. Residents at the school in Battar say three injured people (a man with a crushed foot, a teenage girl with a fractured arm and a woman with burns on her arms), half a dozen schoolchildren, an old man and two uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Residents at the temple in Gerkhu say one adult is still there, sheltering on upper floors. At the health post in Bhalche, three injured people (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle, a child with a deep gash on his leg and an elderly woman with a broken hip), one child, two senior citizens and six uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The Madanpur–Panchakanya road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Debris has completely choked the road between Betrawati and Samundratar. Residents at Kakani say one schoolchild and three able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Gerkhu–Samundratar road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Bhalche–Meghang road; it is open again at normal speed. The Madanpur–Meghang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 15 minutes to the usual journey. The Laharepauwa–Panchakanya road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
