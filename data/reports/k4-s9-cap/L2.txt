FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The Kalikasthan–Thaprek road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 15 minutes to the usual journey. Stranded at Thaprek: three children, an elderly couple and two able-bodied adults. The Madanpur–Thaprek road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. Our team counted four schoolchildren and two uninjured adults at Kalikasthan, all needing evacuation. The Meghang–Tupche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The suspension bridge on the Madanpur–Meghang route was swept away by the river. Hillside collapse: the Madanpur–Tupche road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Everyone from Meghang has already been airlifted out. Residents at the school in Ramche say four schoolchildren, 3 elderly residents and two other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At Tupche, six adults are waiting to be rescued. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
