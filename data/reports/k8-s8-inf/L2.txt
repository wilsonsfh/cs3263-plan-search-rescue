FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The Goljung–Kalikasthan road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Stranded at Thulogaun: one schoolchild and three other adults. The Bageshwari–Thulogaun road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Our team counted three injured people (an old man with a head wound, a woman with burns on her arms and a child with a deep gash on his leg), an elderly couple and two able-bodied adults at Bageshwari, all needing evacuation. Our team counted one child, one senior citizen and four uninjured adults at Ghyangphedi, all needing evacuation. Our team counted three senior citizens and 4 able-bodied adults at Kalikasthan, all needing evacuation. Stranded at the ward office in Likhu: three injured people (a teenage girl with a fractured arm, a child with a deep gash on his leg and a man with a crushed foot), four children and 4 other adults. Residents at Goljung say four children and one uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Suryagadhi was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Bageshwari–Likhu road; it is open again at normal speed. Everyone from Okharpauwa has already been airlifted out. Residents at Belkot say one senior citizen and five uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At the health post in Dupcheshwar, three injured people (a man with a crushed foot, a woman with burns on her arms and a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), four children and four other adults are waiting to be rescued. The Bageshwari–Laharepauwa road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. The suspension bridge on the Kakani–Thulogaun route was swept away by the river. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Ghyangphedi–Goljung road; it is open again at normal speed. Floodwater has turned the road from Ghyangphedi to Laharepauwa into a river. The only bridge linking Kakani and Laharepauwa has collapsed. The Dupcheshwar–Thulogaun road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
