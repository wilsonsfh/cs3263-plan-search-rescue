FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
A landslide has buried the road between Kakani and Thansing under metres of mud. Syaphru was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Hillside collapse: the Bhorle–Trishuli road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Residents at Bhorle say one schoolchild and one uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The suspension bridge on the Bhorle–Kispang route was swept away by the river. Debris has completely choked the road between Kalikasthan and Thansing. Our team counted four schoolchildren, two senior citizens and one able-bodied adult at the school in Thansing, all needing evacuation. Everyone from Kispang has already been airlifted out. Residents at the temple in Trishuli say an old man and 3 able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Bhorle–Kalikasthan road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Residents at Kakani say three schoolchildren, three senior citizens and five other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Bhorle–Kakani road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 15 minutes to the usual journey. Locals warned us about the Lachyang–Syaphru road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
