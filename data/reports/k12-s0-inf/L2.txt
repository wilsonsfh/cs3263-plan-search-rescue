FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Stranded at the temple in Meghang: one injured person (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), 4 children, 3 senior citizens and half a dozen other adults. A landslide has buried the road between Meghang and Salme under metres of mud. Our team counted a baby, one senior citizen and 5 able-bodied adults at Goljung, all needing evacuation. Residents at the school in Haku say three schoolchildren, one elderly resident and one uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Goljung–Likhu road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Locals warned us about the Meghang–Taruka road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. Residents at Thansing say 2 injured people (a woman with burns on her arms and an old man with a head wound), two schoolchildren and one other adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Boulders and fallen trees make the Salme–Sundaradevi road impassable for any vehicle. At Panchakanya, an elderly couple and three uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The Kaule–Salme road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Salme–Taruka road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. At Tupche, two injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris and a child with a deep gash on his leg), a baby, three elderly residents and 4 other adults are waiting to be rescued. The Kumari–Sundaradevi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Stranded at the health post in Kaule: an old man and half a dozen uninjured adults. Residents at the school in Kumari say four schoolchildren and one other adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The suspension bridge on the Kaule–Taruka route was swept away by the river. The Haku–Kumari road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Debris has completely choked the road between Salme and Tadi. Stranded at the school in Sundaradevi: one injured person (an old man with a head wound), nine schoolchildren, two elderly residents and one able-bodied adult. Hillside collapse: the Likhu–Panchakanya road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Locals warned us about the Goljung–Panchakanya road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. Residents at the school in Salme say nine children, an elderly couple and 1 uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Residents at the ward office in Ramche say four children, three senior citizens and one uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Our team counted one schoolchild, one senior citizen and two able-bodied adults at the temple in Tadi, all needing evacuation. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
