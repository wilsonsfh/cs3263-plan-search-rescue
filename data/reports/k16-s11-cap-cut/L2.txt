FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Residents at Rautbesi say one adult is still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at the school in Tadi: three children, one elderly resident and two other adults. The Bhorle–Rautbesi road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The Betrawati–Tupche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Stranded at the school in Sundaradevi: six children, one senior citizen and one other adult. Our team counted four children and 2 uninjured adults at the school in Kispang, all needing evacuation. At Bidur, four schoolchildren, an elderly couple and 3 uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The suspension bridge on the Rautbesi–Tupche route was swept away by the river. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Goljung–Sundaradevi road; it is open again at normal speed. The Bhalche–Thulogaun road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Our team counted one injured person (an elderly woman with a broken hip), three senior citizens and three able-bodied adults at the health post in Meghang, all needing evacuation. The Belkot–Chaughada road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. At Gerkhu, four adults are waiting to be rescued. The Betrawati–Dandagaun road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Boulders and fallen trees make the Belkot–Thulogaun road impassable for any vehicle. Debris has completely choked the road between Gerkhu and Sundaradevi. The Likhu–Meghang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Residents at Dandagaun say four schoolchildren, three elderly residents and six able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Residents at Goljung say three injured people (a man with a crushed foot, a pregnant woman who was hit by debris and a woman with burns on her arms), 2 children, an elderly couple and four able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Residents at Betrawati say two schoolchildren, 3 elderly residents and half a dozen uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Our team counted two adults at Chaughada, all needing evacuation. Stranded at the ward office in Likhu: one child and two uninjured adults. Our team counted 3 injured people (an old man with a head wound, a child with a deep gash on his leg and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), three children and six uninjured adults at Thulogaun, all needing evacuation. Residents at Belkot say one injured person (a child with a deep gash on his leg), two schoolchildren and two other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At Bhorle, two children, an old man and 1 able-bodied adult are waiting to be rescued. At Tupche, three injured people (an old man with a head wound, a teenage girl with a fractured arm and a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), one child, one senior citizen and five able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Hillside collapse: the Madanpur–Rautbesi road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
