FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Our team counted three children, an old man and 2 able-bodied adults at Kaule, all needing evacuation. Our team counted an elderly couple and three uninjured adults at Rautbesi, all needing evacuation. The Ghyangphedi–Haku road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The only bridge linking Ghyangphedi and Panchakanya has collapsed. Stranded at Goljung: three elderly residents and six able-bodied adults. Floodwater has turned the road from Samundratar to Taruka into a river. The Haku–Kaule road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds a quarter of an hour to the usual journey. Stranded at Kakani: an elderly couple and two other adults. The Jiling–Suryagadhi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Everyone from Ghyangphedi has already been airlifted out. A landslide has buried the road between Haku and Taruka under metres of mud. Residents at Jiling say four children, one senior citizen and 1 uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Goljung–Lachyang road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The Bhalche–Tadi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Our team counted two schoolchildren and four uninjured adults at the ward office in Salme, all needing evacuation. The Kakani–Salme road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. At Bhalche, four children and 1 other adult are waiting to be rescued. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Goljung–Jiling road; it is open again at normal speed. Residents at the temple in Betrawati say three children, three elderly residents and 6 uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At Madanpur, an old man and five uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Our team counted two schoolchildren, 2 senior citizens and 4 other adults at the temple in Samundratar, all needing evacuation. The Ghyangphedi–Tadi road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. The Battar–Bhalche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Hillside collapse: the Jiling–Madanpur road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Residents at the temple in Tadi say an old man and one able-bodied adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Residents at Dhaibung say an old man and one able-bodied adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Betrawati–Kakani road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 15 minutes to the usual journey. Residents at Battar say one child, two senior citizens and one uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The suspension bridge on the Betrawati–Kaule route was swept away by the river. Stranded at Panchakanya: 2 children and two able-bodied adults. At Lachyang, a baby and one uninjured adult are waiting to be rescued. Residents at Suryagadhi say one schoolchild and four other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Floodwater has turned the road from Jiling to Panchakanya into a river. Debris has completely choked the road between Lachyang and Panchakanya. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
