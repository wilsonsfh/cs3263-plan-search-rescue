FIELD DISPATCHES (several sources; listed in the order they reached the control room)
[09:30] District Police Office (situation report): The Bhorle–Taruka road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. In Kathmandu, schools have been closed for three days as a precaution. Hillside collapse: the Bageshwari–Taruka road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Stranded at the temple in Thaprek: two children, one elderly resident and two able-bodied adults. Stranded at the school in Suryagadhi: two injured people (an old man with a head wound and a man with a crushed foot), eight children, three elderly residents and two other adults. At the temple in Kakani, an old man and half a dozen able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. At Narjamandap, three schoolchildren, an elderly couple and four uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued.
[13:15] Sunita Lama, The Himalayan Times (on the ground): The Kumari–Taruka road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. The Narjamandap–Taruka road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Residents at Trishuli say a baby and two other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Livestock losses across the district are reported to be severe. A landslide has buried the road between Kakani and Taruka under metres of mud, cutting a road that was open earlier today. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted three schoolchildren and two able-bodied adults at Taruka, all needing evacuation. Confirmed headcount: Stranded at Thansing: three children, three elderly residents and two able-bodied adults. The Bageshwari–Bhorle road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The Thaprek–Trishuli road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Confirmed headcount: Residents at the school in Bageshwari say one injured person (an old man with a head wound), nine schoolchildren, two senior citizens and two uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Boulders and fallen trees make the Kumari–Thansing road impassable for any vehicle.
[06:45] Radio Nuwakot FM (local radio, early bulletin): Debris is blocking the Bageshwari–Bhorle road. Around 9 people are believed to be stranded in Taruka (unconfirmed estimate). The Kakani–Taruka road is still passable this morning, drivers say. Mobile networks remain patchy across the district. Around 11 people are believed to be stranded in Bageshwari (unconfirmed estimate). Around 12 people are believed to be stranded in Thansing (unconfirmed estimate).
Anything not mentioned in these dispatches is in its usual condition, and no one else is known to be stranded.
