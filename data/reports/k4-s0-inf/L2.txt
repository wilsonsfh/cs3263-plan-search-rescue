FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The Meghang–Salme road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Our team counted one injured person (an elderly woman with a broken hip), half a dozen schoolchildren and one uninjured adult at the school in Likhu, all needing evacuation. Debris has completely choked the road between Kispang and Salme. Residents at the health post in Samundratar say 2 adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Kispang–Meghang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Salme–Samundratar road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 15 minutes to the usual journey. Residents at the health post in Meghang say one injured person (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), an elderly couple and four uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Khanigaun–Kispang road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Residents at the temple in Tupche say an old man and 1 able-bodied adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
