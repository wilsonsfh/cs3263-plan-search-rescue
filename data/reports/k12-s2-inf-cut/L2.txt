FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Everyone from Narjamandap has already been airlifted out. At Rautbesi, two injured people (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle and a woman with burns on her arms), one senior citizen and three uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The Betrawati–Shikharbesi road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Stranded at Suryagadhi: four schoolchildren, an old man and six able-bodied adults. At the health post in Likhu, two schoolchildren, three senior citizens and 1 other adult are waiting to be rescued. At Khanigaun, two injured people (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle and a child with a deep gash on his leg), three elderly residents and five able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. At the health post in Thaprek, three children, one elderly resident and two able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. The Kaule–Sundaradevi road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The Rautbesi–Trishuli road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. Stranded at Laharepauwa: two schoolchildren, two elderly residents and two able-bodied adults. Bhalche was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. The Battar–Bhalche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 15 minutes to the usual journey. At the temple in Kaule, one child, one senior citizen and six other adults are waiting to be rescued. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Okharpauwa–Thaprek road; it is open again at normal speed. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Khanigaun–Sundaradevi road; it is open again at normal speed. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Narjamandap–Suryagadhi road; it is open again at normal speed. At the ward office in Okharpauwa, two injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip and a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), 3 children and five able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Stranded at Trishuli: one child and 3 uninjured adults. The Kaule–Shikharbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The suspension bridge on the Kakani–Laharepauwa route was swept away by the river. At the school in Ramche, one injured person (a teenage girl with a fractured arm), 7 schoolchildren and one uninjured adult are waiting to be rescued. Hillside collapse: the Battar–Trishuli road disappeared under a landslide overnight. At Shikharbesi, three senior citizens and 3 uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The Laharepauwa–Ramche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Debris has completely choked the road between Rautbesi and Sundaradevi. Debris has completely choked the road between Khanigaun and Ramche. At Kakani, two children and five able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Boulders and fallen trees make the Betrawati–Kaule road impassable for any vehicle. Locals warned us about the Khanigaun–Okharpauwa road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. Debris has completely choked the road between Bhalche and Okharpauwa. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
