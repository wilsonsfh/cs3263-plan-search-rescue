FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Betrawati was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. The Dandagaun–Panchakanya road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Residents at Tadi say one senior citizen and 5 other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at Bidur: three schoolchildren and five able-bodied adults. The Battar–Thansing road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 15 minutes to the usual journey. The Likhu–Madanpur road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. The Goljung–Meghang road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Goljung was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. At the temple in Likhu, one injured person (a teenage girl with a fractured arm) and four able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Floodwater has turned the road from Bageshwari to Khanigaun into a river. The Bidur–Likhu road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. At the ward office in Rautbesi, half a dozen adults are waiting to be rescued. Stranded at Panchakanya: two adults. Residents at Thulogaun say four schoolchildren, three senior citizens and one able-bodied adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Battar–Ghyangphedi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The Likhu–Rautbesi road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The Dandagaun–Shikharbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Boulders and fallen trees make the Battar–Kakani road impassable for any vehicle. Residents at the temple in Kakani say three injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip, a teenage girl with a fractured arm and a woman with burns on her arms), two children and 2 uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Residents at the ward office in Thansing say two children and three able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at Khanigaun: three senior citizens and half a dozen able-bodied adults. Hillside collapse: the Ghyangphedi–Shikharbesi road disappeared under a landslide overnight. The Bageshwari–Thulogaun road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. At the temple in Dandagaun, an old man and two other adults are waiting to be rescued. Residents at the health post in Meghang say three children, three elderly residents and six uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The only bridge linking Meghang and Tadi has collapsed. Our team counted two injured people (an old man with a head wound and a man with a crushed foot), one senior citizen and two able-bodied adults at Ghyangphedi, all needing evacuation. Our team counted one injured person (an old man with a head wound), two elderly residents and six uninjured adults at Madanpur, all needing evacuation. Our team counted four children, one senior citizen and five uninjured adults at Battar, all needing evacuation. Residents at Bhalche say three injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris, an elderly woman with a broken hip and an old man with a head wound), 2 elderly residents and two able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Residents at the ward office in Chhap say one schoolchild and four other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Goljung–Tadi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
