FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
A landslide has buried the road between Kakani and Kaule under metres of mud. The Kakani–Thansing road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Kalikasthan–Likhu road; it is open again at normal speed. Residents at the temple in Tadi say three schoolchildren, three senior citizens and five able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Residents at the school in Likhu say two injured people (a woman with burns on her arms and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), eight schoolchildren, two elderly residents and two other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Thaprek was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. The Kaule–Thansing road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Hillside collapse: the Thansing–Thulogaun road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Stranded at Dhaibung: four children, three senior citizens and 4 able-bodied adults. At Kakani, three senior citizens and 3 other adults are waiting to be rescued. Locals warned us about the Dhaibung–Likhu road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. At Dandagaun, 3 children, one senior citizen and 3 uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The Dandagaun–Dhaibung road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Locals warned us about the Battar–Dhaibung road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. Floodwater has turned the road from Thansing to Thaprek into a river. The Kalikasthan–Tadi road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. The Khanigaun–Thaprek road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Our team counted two children, 3 senior citizens and three uninjured adults at the ward office in Thulogaun, all needing evacuation. Our team counted 3 injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip, an old man with a head wound and a teenage girl with a fractured arm), a baby, three elderly residents and 6 able-bodied adults at the ward office in Battar, all needing evacuation. Stranded at Kaule: two injured people (a man with a crushed foot and an elderly woman with a broken hip), three schoolchildren and five uninjured adults. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
