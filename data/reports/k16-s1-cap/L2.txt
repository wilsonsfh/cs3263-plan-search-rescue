FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Stranded at Goljung: 4 adults. Stranded at Kispang: three senior citizens and 1 uninjured adult. Residents at Thansing say two schoolchildren and four uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Our team counted three children and four other adults at Syaphru, all needing evacuation. Our team counted a baby, an elderly couple and two able-bodied adults at Ramche, all needing evacuation. A landslide has buried the road between Kispang and Kumari under metres of mud. Our team counted eight schoolchildren and one other adult at the school in Thaprek, all needing evacuation. The Samundratar–Thaprek road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Stranded at the school in Kakani: four children and one able-bodied adult. Our team counted an elderly couple and three uninjured adults at Chhap, all needing evacuation. The suspension bridge on the Bhorle–Ramche route was swept away by the river. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Salme–Thansing road; it is open again at normal speed. The only bridge linking Belkot and Salme has collapsed. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Goljung–Khanigaun road; it is open again at normal speed. Boulders and fallen trees make the Haku–Madanpur road impassable for any vehicle. The Kumari–Ramche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Chhap–Panchakanya road; it is open again at normal speed. The Kaule–Khanigaun road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. Our team counted one injured person (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), one schoolchild and five other adults at Kaule, all needing evacuation. Our team counted one schoolchild, an elderly couple and four able-bodied adults at the temple in Dandagaun, all needing evacuation. The only bridge linking Belkot and Goljung has collapsed. At Haku, six adults are waiting to be rescued. Our team counted 3 adults at the health post in Belkot, all needing evacuation. At the temple in Salme, a baby, three senior citizens and two other adults are waiting to be rescued. The Likhu–Ramche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Betrawati–Sundaradevi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Floodwater has turned the road from Kakani to Sundaradevi into a river. Our team counted two schoolchildren, one senior citizen and 3 uninjured adults at Kumari, all needing evacuation. At the ward office in Bhorle, three injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip, an old man with a head wound and a teenage girl with a fractured arm) and three other adults are waiting to be rescued. The Belkot–Kaule road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Stranded at Madanpur: three schoolchildren, 3 senior citizens and three able-bodied adults. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
