FIELD DISPATCHES (several sources; listed in the order they reached the control room)
[06:45] Radio Nuwakot FM (local radio, early bulletin): The Bhorle–Salme road is still passable this morning, drivers say. Residents of Samundratar were seen on rooftops last night. Around 4 people are believed to be stranded in Narjamandap (unconfirmed estimate). The Kispang–Likhu road is still passable this morning, drivers say. Livestock losses across the district are reported to be severe. Residents of Jiling were seen on rooftops last night.
[09:30] District Police Office (situation report): Stranded at Salme: 2 elderly residents and four other adults. The Home Ministry says relief funds will be released next week. A landslide has buried the road between Bhorle and Samundratar under metres of mud. Unconfirmed posts on social media claim the Narjamandap–Salme road has been washed away.
[13:15] Sunita Lama, The Himalayan Times (on the ground): The Narjamandap–Samundratar road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Police have checked the Narjamandap–Salme road: the social media reports were false and the road is open as normal. Everyone from Samundratar has already been airlifted out. Hillside collapse: the Kispang–Likhu road disappeared under a landslide overnight, cutting a road that was open earlier today. Floodwater has turned the road from Bhorle to Likhu into a river. Everyone from Jiling has already been airlifted out. Confirmed headcount: At Narjamandap, an elderly couple and five uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. At Likhu, one injured person (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), four children, an old man and 3 able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. At the health post in Kispang, three injured people (a teenage girl with a fractured arm, a man with a crushed foot and a woman with burns on her arms) and 1 other adult are waiting to be rescued. Hillside collapse: the Bhorle–Salme road disappeared under a landslide overnight, cutting a road that was open earlier today. Heavy rain is forecast to continue through the evening. The Bhorle–Jiling road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey.
Anything not mentioned in these dispatches is in its usual condition, and no one else is known to be stranded.
