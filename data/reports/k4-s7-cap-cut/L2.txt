FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Locals warned us about the Narjamandap–Salme road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. A landslide has buried the road between Kispang and Likhu under metres of mud. Residents at Narjamandap say two senior citizens and five other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Samundratar was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Stranded at Salme: two elderly residents and 4 able-bodied adults. Floodwater has turned the road from Bhorle to Likhu into a river. Residents at the health post in Kispang say 3 injured people (a man with a crushed foot, a young boy with a badly sprained ankle and an old man with a head wound) and one able-bodied adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Bhorle–Jiling road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. The Narjamandap–Samundratar road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Everyone from Jiling has already been airlifted out. Our team counted one injured person (an elderly woman with a broken hip), 4 children, an old man and three uninjured adults at Likhu, all needing evacuation. A landslide has buried the road between Bhorle and Salme under metres of mud. Hillside collapse: the Bhorle–Samundratar road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
