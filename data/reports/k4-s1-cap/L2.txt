FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
At the temple in Lachyang, three children and one other adult are waiting to be rescued. Everyone from Dupcheshwar has already been airlifted out. Stranded at Madanpur: one child, two senior citizens and three able-bodied adults. Stranded at the health post in Chhap: two injured people (a child with a deep gash on his leg and a woman with burns on her arms), 4 schoolchildren and 4 uninjured adults. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Battar–Chhap road; it is open again at normal speed. The Chhap–Lachyang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The suspension bridge on the Battar–Chaughada route was swept away by the river. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Chaughada–Madanpur road; it is open again at normal speed. The Chaughada–Chhap road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. At the temple in Battar, 3 injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris, an elderly woman with a broken hip and a woman with burns on her arms), four schoolchildren, one elderly resident and five able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. The Battar–Dupcheshwar road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
