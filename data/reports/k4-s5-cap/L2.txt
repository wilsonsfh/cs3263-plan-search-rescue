FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The suspension bridge on the Taruka–Thaprek route was swept away by the river. The Likhu–Sundaradevi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The Chaughada–Thaprek road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The Chaughada–Likhu road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Residents at Thaprek say one injured person (a teenage girl with a fractured arm), two children, an elderly couple and five uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Suryagadhi–Taruka road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Our team counted 3 children, three senior citizens and two other adults at the temple in Likhu, all needing evacuation. At Chaughada, two schoolchildren, an old man and one uninjured adult are waiting to be rescued. Samundratar was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Stranded at Suryagadhi: two senior citizens and five other adults. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
