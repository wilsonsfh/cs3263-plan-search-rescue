FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The Betrawati–Madanpur road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Residents at Sundaradevi say two elderly residents and 3 other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Likhu–Sundaradevi road; it is open again at normal speed. At Thulogaun, one child and two able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Residents at Kispang say six adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Dandagaun was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Residents at Shikharbesi say three schoolchildren and 1 uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Bageshwari–Likhu road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. The Bageshwari–Samundratar road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Boulders and fallen trees make the Madanpur–Thulogaun road impassable for any vehicle. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Kumari–Likhu road; it is open again at normal speed. At Panchakanya, three adults are waiting to be rescued. Stranded at Betrawati: five adults. The Ghyangphedi–Kispang road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Ramche–Thaprek road; it is open again at normal speed. The only bridge linking Likhu and Samundratar has collapsed. The Samundratar–Sundaradevi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Haku–Shikharbesi road; it is open again at normal speed. A landslide has buried the road between Laharepauwa and Thaprek under metres of mud. Locals warned us about the Haku–Kispang road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. Floodwater has turned the road from Betrawati to Ghyangphedi into a river. Residents at the school in Laharepauwa say two injured people (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle and a teenage girl with a fractured arm), half a dozen children, three elderly residents and 1 uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at Likhu: two schoolchildren and 5 other adults. At the temple in Kumari, one injured person (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle) and half a dozen able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Boulders and fallen trees make the Likhu–Trishuli road impassable for any vehicle. Residents at Haku say 4 children, one senior citizen and two other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Residents at Samundratar say two adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Everyone from Bageshwari has already been airlifted out. Residents at Madanpur say three senior citizens and five uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
