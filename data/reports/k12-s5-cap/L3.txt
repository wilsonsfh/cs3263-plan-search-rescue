FIELD DISPATCHES (several sources; listed in the order they reached the control room)
[13:15] Sunita Lama, The Himalayan Times (on the ground): The Kumari–Likhu road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Stranded at Thulogaun: one schoolchild and two uninjured adults. Bageshwari was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Police have checked the Haku–Kispang road: the social media reports were false and the road is open as normal. Confirmed headcount: Stranded at Sundaradevi: an elderly couple and three other adults. The Betrawati–Madanpur road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Ramche–Thaprek road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Police have checked the Ghyangphedi–Kispang road: the social media reports were false and the road is open as normal. Confirmed headcount: Stranded at Panchakanya: 3 adults. The Betrawati–Ghyangphedi road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater, cutting a road that was open earlier today. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Haku–Shikharbesi road; it is open again at normal speed. Confirmed headcount: At Samundratar, two adults are waiting to be rescued. Dandagaun was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Debris has completely choked the road between Likhu and Trishuli. The Bageshwari–Likhu road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. The suspension bridge on the Likhu–Samundratar route was swept away by the river. Livestock losses across the district are reported to be severe. Confirmed headcount: Residents at the school in Laharepauwa say 2 injured people (a child with a deep gash on his leg and an elderly woman with a broken hip), six children, three elderly residents and one able-bodied adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Likhu–Sundaradevi road; it is open again at normal speed. Confirmed headcount: Residents at Shikharbesi say 3 children and one uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors.

At the temple in Kumari, one injured person (a teenage girl with a fractured arm) and 6 uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Boulders and fallen trees make the Madanpur–Thulogaun road impassable for any vehicle.
