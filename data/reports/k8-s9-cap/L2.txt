FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The only bridge linking Dupcheshwar and Samundratar has collapsed. The Chaughada–Suryagadhi road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. The Dupcheshwar–Narjamandap road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Our team counted 4 schoolchildren and two other adults at the school in Sundaradevi, all needing evacuation. The Chaughada–Sundaradevi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. At Jiling, one schoolchild and three uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The Chaughada–Narjamandap road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Our team counted one injured person (a man with a crushed foot), four children and 6 uninjured adults at Suryagadhi, all needing evacuation. Our team counted one adult at the temple in Meghang, all needing evacuation. A landslide has buried the road between Jiling and Suryagadhi under metres of mud. Stranded at Dupcheshwar: one schoolchild and 2 other adults. Stranded at Trishuli: three injured people (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle, a man with a crushed foot and a child with a deep gash on his leg), two schoolchildren and 5 uninjured adults. The Jiling–Trishuli road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Stranded at Narjamandap: three adults. Residents at Samundratar say one injured person (an old man with a head wound), a baby, an old man and two other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Suryagadhi–Trishuli road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The Dupcheshwar–Kumari road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
