FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The Dupcheshwar–Kumari road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Residents at Tupche say two senior citizens and 2 uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at Thaprek: four children and five able-bodied adults. Debris has completely choked the road between Kumari and Panchakanya. Our team counted four children and 6 uninjured adults at Chhap, all needing evacuation. The Battar–Panchakanya road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Everyone from Rautbesi has already been airlifted out. Everyone from Panchakanya has already been airlifted out. The only bridge linking Betrawati and Thansing has collapsed. At Meghang, one elderly resident and 4 other adults are waiting to be rescued. Stranded at Likhu: four adults. Thansing was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. The Dupcheshwar–Meghang road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Floodwater has turned the road from Meghang to Panchakanya into a river. The Bageshwari–Gerkhu road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Residents at the temple in Narjamandap say half a dozen adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Battar–Betrawati road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Residents at the school in Dupcheshwar say one injured person (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), three schoolchildren, two senior citizens and two other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Residents at the school in Salme say five children and two other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The suspension bridge on the Narjamandap–Salme route was swept away by the river. Our team counted one injured person (a woman with burns on her arms), 2 elderly residents and three other adults at Bageshwari, all needing evacuation. The only bridge linking Bhorle and Salme has collapsed. Stranded at Gerkhu: one injured person (a teenage girl with a fractured arm), one elderly resident and four able-bodied adults. The Bageshwari–Narjamandap road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Stranded at the health post in Betrawati: 2 injured people (an old man with a head wound and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), three schoolchildren and five uninjured adults. Our team counted two injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), three senior citizens and five able-bodied adults at Bhorle, all needing evacuation. The Rautbesi–Thaprek road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
