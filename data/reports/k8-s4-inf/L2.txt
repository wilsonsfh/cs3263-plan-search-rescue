FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Residents at the health post in Okharpauwa say a baby, an elderly couple and three able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at the school in Tupche: eight children, one senior citizen and 2 other adults. Stranded at the ward office in Taruka: four adults. The Chaughada–Lachyang road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Our team counted one injured person (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), one child and four other adults at Chaughada, all needing evacuation. Residents at the school in Chhap say three injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip, a woman with burns on her arms and a man with a crushed foot), 5 schoolchildren and two uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Meghang–Taruka road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. At Goljung, 2 injured people (a man with a crushed foot and an old man with a head wound), one schoolchild, one elderly resident and 4 uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The suspension bridge on the Lachyang–Meghang route was swept away by the river. Residents at Shikharbesi say four children and six other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Chhap–Tupche road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Betrawati was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Debris has completely choked the road between Okharpauwa and Taruka. The Goljung–Lachyang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Stranded at Lachyang: a baby and five other adults. The Goljung–Okharpauwa road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
