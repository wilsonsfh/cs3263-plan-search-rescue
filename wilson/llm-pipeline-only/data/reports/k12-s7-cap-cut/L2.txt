FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Residents at Taruka say one injured person (a child with a deep gash on his leg), a baby and one uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Debris has completely choked the road between Chaughada and Kumari. The Kumari–Meghang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Residents at Thaprek say one injured person (an elderly woman with a broken hip), two schoolchildren, an old man and three uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At Kumari, one child, two senior citizens and three able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Our team counted two injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris and a woman with burns on her arms), four schoolchildren, three senior citizens and five other adults at the health post in Bageshwari, all needing evacuation. A landslide has buried the road between Battar and Salme under metres of mud. Residents at Laharepauwa say one injured person (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), four schoolchildren, 2 senior citizens and 5 uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at Ramche: 3 schoolchildren, one senior citizen and four uninjured adults. Stranded at the school in Trishuli: 4 children and 1 uninjured adult. At the ward office in Meghang, three injured people (a woman with burns on her arms, an elderly woman with a broken hip and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), two children, 3 senior citizens and three uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Our team counted three injured people (a teenage girl with a fractured arm, a pregnant woman who was hit by debris and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), three elderly residents and 4 uninjured adults at Salme, all needing evacuation. The Bidur–Shikharbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Salme–Trishuli road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. A landslide has buried the road between Chaughada and Haku under metres of mud. Floodwater has turned the road from Kaule to Syaphru into a river. Everyone from Chaughada has already been airlifted out. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Bageshwari–Laharepauwa road; it is open again at normal speed. The only bridge linking Ramche and Salme has collapsed. Residents at the health post in Bidur say three injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris, a young boy with a badly sprained ankle and a teenage girl with a fractured arm), 2 elderly residents and three other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Meghang–Trishuli road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. The Haku–Taruka road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. At Kaule, four children and four able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. At the health post in Syaphru, a baby and five other adults are waiting to be rescued. Everyone from Battar has already been airlifted out. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
