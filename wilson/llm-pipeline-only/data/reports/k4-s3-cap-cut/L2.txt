FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The Bhorle–Shikharbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Stranded at Ramche: 6 adults. The Bhorle–Ramche road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. The Kaule–Thaprek road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Floodwater has turned the road from Khanigaun to Ramche into a river. The only bridge linking Chaughada and Shikharbesi has collapsed. The Chaughada–Trishuli road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. Stranded at the ward office in Khanigaun: 2 elderly residents and 3 other adults. A landslide has buried the road between Bhorle and Trishuli under metres of mud. At the health post in Trishuli, one injured person (a woman with burns on her arms), two schoolchildren, three elderly residents and three able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Residents at Shikharbesi say one injured person (an elderly woman with a broken hip), four schoolchildren, an old man and two able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Bhorle was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
