FIELD DISPATCHES (several sources; listed in the order they reached the control room)
[13:15] Sunita Lama, The Himalayan Times (on the ground): Stranded at Thansing: two injured people (a man with a crushed foot and a teenage girl with a fractured arm), two children and 1 able-bodied adult. Confirmed headcount: Residents at Tupche say two injured people (a man with a crushed foot and a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), a baby, three elderly residents and four uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted four schoolchildren and one uninjured adult at the school in Kumari, all needing evacuation. The Kaule–Salme road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Debris has completely choked the road between Salme and Tadi, cutting a road that was open earlier today. Stranded at the health post in Kaule: one senior citizen and half a dozen other adults. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Haku–Kumari road; it is open again at normal speed. Mobile networks remain patchy across the district. Confirmed headcount: Stranded at the ward office in Ramche: four children, three senior citizens and one uninjured adult. Confirmed headcount: At Panchakanya, an elderly couple and three uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Residents at the school in Salme say 9 schoolchildren, 2 senior citizens and one uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Police have checked the Meghang–Taruka road: the social media reports were false and the road is open as normal. Our team counted one injured person (a child with a deep gash on his leg), nine children, an elderly couple and one able-bodied adult at the school in Sundaradevi, all needing evacuation. Police have checked the Goljung–Panchakanya road: the social media reports were false and the road is open as normal. A landslide has buried the road between Meghang and Salme under metres of mud. Hillside collapse: the Likhu–Panchakanya road disappeared under a landslide overnight. The suspension bridge on the Kaule–Taruka route was swept away by the river. At Goljung, a baby, an old man and 5 able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued.

Around 3 people are believed to be stranded in Kumari (unconfirmed estimate). Around 7 people are believed to be stranded in Tupche (unconfirmed estimate). Around 8 people are believed to be stranded in Panchakanya (unconfirmed estimate). The Salme–Tadi road is still passable this morning, drivers say. Around 11 people are believed to be stranded in Ramche (unconfirmed estimate).
