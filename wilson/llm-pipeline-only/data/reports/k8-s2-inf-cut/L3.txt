FIELD DISPATCHES (several sources; listed in the order they reached the control room)
[13:15] Sunita Lama, The Himalayan Times (on the ground): The Bidur–Madanpur road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Confirmed headcount: At the ward office in Bhalche, one senior citizen and five uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Boulders and fallen trees make the Dhaibung–Kumari road impassable for any vehicle, cutting a road that was open earlier today. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted four children, two elderly residents and 5 other adults at Chaughada, all needing evacuation. Everyone from Likhu has already been airlifted out. Mobile networks remain patchy across the district. Confirmed headcount: Residents at the health post in Dhaibung say two adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Confirmed headcount: Stranded at the school in Bidur: one injured person (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), five schoolchildren and two uninjured adults. Police have checked the Kumari–Ramche road: the social media reports were false and the road is open as normal. The Bhalche–Taruka road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The suspension bridge on the Bidur–Chaughada route was swept away by the river, cutting a road that was open earlier today. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted one schoolchild, three senior citizens and six uninjured adults at Ramche, all needing evacuation. The only bridge linking Chaughada and Thansing has collapsed. At Kumari, 3 children and six uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The suspension bridge on the Bidur–Taruka route was swept away by the river, cutting a road that was open earlier today. Confirmed headcount: Stranded at the ward office in Madanpur: a baby, one elderly resident and four other adults.
[06:45] Radio Nuwakot FM (local radio, early bulletin): Residents of Likhu were seen on rooftops last night. Around 10 people are believed to be stranded in Madanpur (unconfirmed estimate). Around 7 people are believed to be stranded in Ramche (unconfirmed estimate). The Dhaibung–Kumari road is still passable this morning, drivers say. Debris is blocking the Bhalche–Taruka road. The Bidur–Taruka road is still passable this morning, drivers say. A government helicopter was seen flying north this morning but did not land. Around 5 people are believed to be stranded in Dhaibung (unconfirmed estimate). Around 9 people are believed to be stranded in Chaughada (unconfirmed estimate). Around 12 people are believed to be stranded in Bidur (unconfirmed estimate). The Bidur–Chaughada road is still passable this morning, drivers say. Around 9 people are believed to be stranded in Bhalche (unconfirmed estimate).
[09:30] District Police Office (situation report): The Dhaibung–Likhu road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Heavy rain is forecast to continue through the evening. The Bhalche–Madanpur road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds a quarter of an hour to the usual journey. Debris has completely choked the road between Bhalche and Chaughada. Unconfirmed posts on social media claim the Kumari–Ramche road has been washed away. Stranded at Taruka: three injured people (a woman with burns on her arms, an elderly woman with a broken hip and a teenage girl with a fractured arm), three schoolchildren and five other adults. Hillside collapse: the Ramche–Thansing road disappeared under a landslide overnight.
Anything not mentioned in these dispatches is in its usual condition, and no one else is known to be stranded.
