FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Our team counted an elderly couple and three uninjured adults at Lachyang, all needing evacuation. Residents at Chhap say two schoolchildren, one senior citizen and four other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Kaule–Thaprek road; it is open again at normal speed. At Taruka, three senior citizens and half a dozen able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Floodwater has turned the road from Kumari to Thaprek into a river. Hillside collapse: the Betrawati–Ramche road disappeared under a landslide overnight. The Lachyang–Shikharbesi road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Stranded at the temple in Chaughada: two injured people (a teenage girl with a fractured arm and an old man with a head wound), three children, two elderly residents and three other adults. The Ramche–Shikharbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Belkot–Khanigaun road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Hillside collapse: the Lachyang–Sundaradevi road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Floodwater has turned the road from Betrawati to Taruka into a river. At the temple in Dhaibung, one injured person (an old man with a head wound) and one other adult are waiting to be rescued. Our team counted 5 adults at the temple in Tupche, all needing evacuation. The Shikharbesi–Tupche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. At the temple in Ramche, two injured people (a man with a crushed foot and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle) and four other adults are waiting to be rescued. Residents at the ward office in Laharepauwa say three injured people (an old man with a head wound, a young boy with a badly sprained ankle and a woman with burns on her arms) and two uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At Kaule, one elderly resident and one able-bodied adult are waiting to be rescued. Our team counted two injured people (a child with a deep gash on his leg and a teenage girl with a fractured arm), four children and five other adults at Belkot, all needing evacuation. The Shikharbesi–Sundaradevi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. The Jiling–Sundaradevi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Our team counted three injured people (a child with a deep gash on his leg, an elderly woman with a broken hip and a teenage girl with a fractured arm), three elderly residents and half a dozen other adults at the temple in Kumari, all needing evacuation. Stranded at the ward office in Shikharbesi: 3 elderly residents and half a dozen able-bodied adults. Stranded at Khanigaun: one schoolchild and 2 other adults. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Kaule–Laharepauwa road; it is open again at normal speed. Residents at Sundaradevi say an elderly couple and 3 able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Betrawati–Tupche road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Stranded at the temple in Okharpauwa: two children, two senior citizens and five able-bodied adults. Residents at the temple in Betrawati say four children and 3 other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
