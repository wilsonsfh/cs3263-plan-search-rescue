FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Our team counted a baby, two senior citizens and one able-bodied adult at Salme, all needing evacuation. Floodwater has turned the road from Dupcheshwar to Kispang into a river. The Chaughada–Syaphru road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. The suspension bridge on the Khanigaun–Rautbesi route was swept away by the river. The Syaphru–Taruka road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Our team counted 4 schoolchildren, one elderly resident and half a dozen other adults at Bhalche, all needing evacuation. Residents at the health post in Likhu say 3 injured people (an old man with a head wound, an elderly woman with a broken hip and a child with a deep gash on his leg), three elderly residents and 5 uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Hillside collapse: the Likhu–Ramche road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Stranded at Khanigaun: 4 schoolchildren, three senior citizens and six other adults. Our team counted 2 schoolchildren and 4 uninjured adults at Dandagaun, all needing evacuation. Residents at Dupcheshwar say one injured person (an elderly woman with a broken hip) and four able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Our team counted an old man and one uninjured adult at Jiling, all needing evacuation. The Jiling–Laharepauwa road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Our team counted one injured person (an elderly woman with a broken hip), two children and six uninjured adults at the health post in Taruka, all needing evacuation. The suspension bridge on the Bhalche–Taruka route was swept away by the river. Residents at Syaphru say three injured people (a woman with burns on her arms, a young boy with a badly sprained ankle and an old man with a head wound), one schoolchild, three elderly residents and four other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Debris has completely choked the road between Chaughada and Dandagaun. Stranded at Rautbesi: three injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip, a teenage girl with a fractured arm and a child with a deep gash on his leg), a baby and five uninjured adults. At the health post in Kalikasthan, one injured person (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), two elderly residents and two able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Our team counted three injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip, a child with a deep gash on his leg and a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), three children and two able-bodied adults at the temple in Kispang, all needing evacuation. The Dandagaun–Salme road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The Kalikasthan–Rautbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
