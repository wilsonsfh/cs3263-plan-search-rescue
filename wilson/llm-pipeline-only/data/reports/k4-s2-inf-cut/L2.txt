FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
A landslide has buried the road between Goljung and Syaphru under metres of mud. Everyone from Kakani has already been airlifted out. Hillside collapse: the Panchakanya–Trishuli road disappeared under a landslide overnight. At the health post in Battar, one injured person (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), three schoolchildren and four uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Trishuli was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Residents at Bageshwari say one injured person (a teenage girl with a fractured arm), three children, 2 elderly residents and four other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Bageshwari–Syaphru road; it is open again at normal speed. The Battar–Kakani road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. A landslide has buried the road between Goljung and Trishuli under metres of mud. The only bridge linking Kakani and Panchakanya has collapsed. Our team counted four children and six uninjured adults at the temple in Panchakanya, all needing evacuation. The Syaphru–Trishuli road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Residents at Syaphru say three injured people (a woman with burns on her arms, a man with a crushed foot and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), three children and four other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
