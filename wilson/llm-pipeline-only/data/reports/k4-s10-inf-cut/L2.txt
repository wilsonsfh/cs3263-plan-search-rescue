FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Stranded at Laharepauwa: one injured person (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle) and three able-bodied adults. Betrawati was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Boulders and fallen trees make the Betrawati–Shikharbesi road impassable for any vehicle. Chhap was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. The Bageshwari–Bhorle road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. The Samundratar–Shikharbesi road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. The Chhap–Laharepauwa road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Samundratar was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Stranded at Sundaradevi: one schoolchild and three uninjured adults. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Bhorle–Chhap road; it is open again at normal speed. At Bhorle, 2 children, three senior citizens and four other adults are waiting to be rescued. The Chhap–Sundaradevi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The Bhorle–Sundaradevi road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Stranded at Shikharbesi: a baby and four other adults. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Bageshwari–Betrawati road; it is open again at normal speed. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
