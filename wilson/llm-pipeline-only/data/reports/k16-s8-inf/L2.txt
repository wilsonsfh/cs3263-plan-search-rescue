FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Our team counted a baby, three senior citizens and one able-bodied adult at Madanpur, all needing evacuation. Our team counted an old man and three other adults at Ghyangphedi, all needing evacuation. Stranded at Tadi: one adult. Residents at Betrawati say an old man and one uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at Haku: 2 adults. At the temple in Laharepauwa, three injured people (a child with a deep gash on his leg, an old man with a head wound and an elderly woman with a broken hip), a baby and 6 other adults are waiting to be rescued. A landslide has buried the road between Kalikasthan and Madanpur under metres of mud. Residents at the ward office in Sundaradevi say one child and two uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Belkot–Haku road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Our team counted nine children, one senior citizen and two other adults at the school in Shikharbesi, all needing evacuation. The Betrawati–Kalikasthan road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Kispang–Syaphru road; it is open again at normal speed. The suspension bridge on the Ghyangphedi–Jiling route was swept away by the river. Our team counted three injured people (a child with a deep gash on his leg, a man with a crushed foot and an elderly woman with a broken hip), three schoolchildren, an elderly couple and six uninjured adults at Bhalche, all needing evacuation. Our team counted 4 children, 3 elderly residents and two other adults at the school in Kispang, all needing evacuation. Our team counted three children, three senior citizens and two other adults at the temple in Kakani, all needing evacuation. The Kispang–Laharepauwa road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Residents at the health post in Suryagadhi say 2 children, three senior citizens and five other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Debris has completely choked the road between Belkot and Jiling. Battar was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Our team counted four schoolchildren and 6 able-bodied adults at Belkot, all needing evacuation. The Kaule–Rautbesi road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. Locals warned us about the Kaule–Laharepauwa road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. A landslide has buried the road between Haku and Kaule under metres of mud. Everyone from Kaule has already been airlifted out. The Ghyangphedi–Shikharbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The Haku–Rautbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds a quarter of an hour to the usual journey. Stranded at the health post in Kalikasthan: one injured person (a woman with burns on her arms), one schoolchild, one elderly resident and five able-bodied adults. Stranded at Syaphru: three senior citizens and 5 uninjured adults. The Belkot–Bhalche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. At Rautbesi, two children and five uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Debris has completely choked the road between Battar and Haku. The Syaphru–Tadi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
