FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
At Khanigaun, four adults are waiting to be rescued. The Madanpur–Salme road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 15 minutes to the usual journey. The only bridge linking Kispang and Kumari has collapsed. The Madanpur–Taruka road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. At Dupcheshwar, four children, two elderly residents and three uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The Haku–Tadi road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Residents at Salme say two schoolchildren, two senior citizens and five able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at Tadi: two children and 5 uninjured adults. Our team counted two schoolchildren, three senior citizens and 6 able-bodied adults at Shikharbesi, all needing evacuation. At Haku, four children, three elderly residents and one able-bodied adult are waiting to be rescued. Stranded at the health post in Ghyangphedi: two injured people (a child with a deep gash on his leg and a woman with burns on her arms), one schoolchild and five other adults. The Dupcheshwar–Kalikasthan road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The Dandagaun–Taruka road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Our team counted one injured person (a man with a crushed foot), 2 schoolchildren and six uninjured adults at Kalikasthan, all needing evacuation. Residents at the temple in Dhaibung say 2 schoolchildren and four able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Floodwater has turned the road from Salme to Taruka into a river. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Bageshwari–Shikharbesi road; it is open again at normal speed. At the ward office in Battar, two injured people (a woman with burns on her arms and an elderly woman with a broken hip), one elderly resident and three able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Stranded at Lachyang: one injured person (a teenage girl with a fractured arm), an old man and two uninjured adults. The Ramche–Tadi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 15 minutes to the usual journey. A landslide has buried the road between Dhaibung and Meghang under metres of mud. At Dandagaun, five adults are waiting to be rescued. Our team counted one injured person (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), three schoolchildren and three uninjured adults at Ramche, all needing evacuation. Hillside collapse: the Bageshwari–Kispang road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Kalikasthan–Khanigaun road; it is open again at normal speed. At Kispang, two injured people (a teenage girl with a fractured arm and a man with a crushed foot), two elderly residents and 4 uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The suspension bridge on the Bageshwari–Dandagaun route was swept away by the river. Residents at Kumari say four children, three elderly residents and half a dozen uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Battar–Tadi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Dandagaun–Madanpur road; it is open again at normal speed. At the ward office in Thansing, two children, 3 senior citizens and two uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
