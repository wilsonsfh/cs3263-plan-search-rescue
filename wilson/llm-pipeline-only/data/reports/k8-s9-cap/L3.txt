FIELD DISPATCHES (several sources; listed in the order they reached the control room)
[06:45] Radio Nuwakot FM (local radio, early bulletin): Around 3 people are believed to be stranded in Meghang (unconfirmed estimate). Debris is blocking the Jiling–Trishuli road. Livestock losses across the district are reported to be severe. Around 6 people are believed to be stranded in Dupcheshwar (unconfirmed estimate).
[13:15] Sunita Lama, The Himalayan Times (on the ground): Confirmed headcount: Residents at the temple in Meghang say one adult is still there, sheltering on upper floors. Residents at Suryagadhi say one injured person (a man with a crushed foot), four children and half a dozen able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Dupcheshwar–Narjamandap road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Stranded at the school in Sundaradevi: four children and two other adults. At Trishuli, three injured people (an old man with a head wound, a child with a deep gash on his leg and an elderly woman with a broken hip), two schoolchildren and five able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. A government helicopter was seen flying north this morning but did not land. The Jiling–Trishuli road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Confirmed headcount: Stranded at Dupcheshwar: one child and 2 uninjured adults. A landslide has buried the road between Jiling and Suryagadhi under metres of mud. Residents at Narjamandap say three adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Police have checked the Chaughada–Suryagadhi road: the social media reports were false and the road is open as normal. The Chaughada–Narjamandap road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Police have checked the Dupcheshwar–Kumari road: the social media reports were false and the road is open as normal.
[09:30] District Police Office (situation report): The Suryagadhi–Trishuli road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. In Kathmandu, schools have been closed for three days as a precaution. The Chaughada–Sundaradevi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Our team counted one child and three able-bodied adults at Jiling, all needing evacuation. At Samundratar, one injured person (a woman with burns on her arms), a baby, one elderly resident and two uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Unconfirmed posts on social media claim the Dupcheshwar–Kumari road has been washed away. The only bridge linking Dupcheshwar and Samundratar has collapsed. Unconfirmed posts on social media claim the Chaughada–Suryagadhi road has been washed away.
Anything not mentioned in these dispatches is in its usual condition, and no one else is known to be stranded.
