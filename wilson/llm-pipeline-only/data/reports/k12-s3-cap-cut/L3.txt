Residents at Kalikasthan say three injured people (a man with a crushed foot, a young boy with a badly sprained ankle and an elderly woman with a broken hip), one child and one uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Mobile networks remain patchy across the district. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted three injured people (a woman with burns on her arms, a teenage girl with a fractured arm and an elderly woman with a broken hip), one schoolchild, an old man and half a dozen able-bodied adults at the health post in Chaughada, all needing evacuation. Syaphru was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Confirmed headcount: At Ramche, two injured people (a woman with burns on her arms and a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), four schoolchildren, three senior citizens and 3 uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued.
