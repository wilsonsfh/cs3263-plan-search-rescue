FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Hillside collapse: the Jiling–Kalikasthan road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Stranded at Shikharbesi: 2 injured people (a man with a crushed foot and an old man with a head wound), two children, one senior citizen and two other adults. Everyone from Goljung has already been airlifted out. The Bhorle–Haku road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The Chaughada–Laharepauwa road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. The Bhorle–Rautbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Our team counted a baby, 2 senior citizens and two able-bodied adults at the temple in Haku, all needing evacuation. The Haku–Kispang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Syaphru was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Stranded at the health post in Chaughada: three injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris, a teenage girl with a fractured arm and a man with a crushed foot), a baby, one senior citizen and 6 other adults. The Goljung–Sundaradevi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Stranded at the temple in Rautbesi: four children and two other adults. Our team counted four schoolchildren, an old man and one other adult at Kispang, all needing evacuation. Our team counted two injured people (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle and a woman with burns on her arms), four children, 3 elderly residents and three uninjured adults at Ramche, all needing evacuation. Our team counted three injured people (a teenage girl with a fractured arm, a child with a deep gash on his leg and a woman with burns on her arms), three senior citizens and six uninjured adults at Bhorle, all needing evacuation. The Ramche–Shikharbesi road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. Locals warned us about the Goljung–Ramche road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. Hillside collapse: the Kalikasthan–Kispang road disappeared under a landslide overnight. The Rautbesi–Syaphru road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Laharepauwa–Sundaradevi road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. At Okharpauwa, two children and three able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. The suspension bridge on the Okharpauwa–Shikharbesi route was swept away by the river. At Sundaradevi, one child, 3 elderly residents and 6 other adults are waiting to be rescued. Debris has completely choked the road between Jiling and Sundaradevi. A landslide has buried the road between Jiling and Laharepauwa under metres of mud. Stranded at the ward office in Khanigaun: two senior citizens and five uninjured adults. At Salme, one injured person (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), 3 elderly residents and 4 uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Stranded at Kalikasthan: three injured people (a man with a crushed foot, a woman with burns on her arms and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), one child and 1 uninjured adult. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
