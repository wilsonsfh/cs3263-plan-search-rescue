FIELD DISPATCHES (several sources; listed in the order they reached the control room)
[06:45] Radio Nuwakot FM (local radio, early bulletin): Around 9 people are believed to be stranded in Dandagaun (unconfirmed estimate). Around 11 people are believed to be stranded in Battar (unconfirmed estimate). Around 11 people are believed to be stranded in Belkot (unconfirmed estimate). A government helicopter was seen flying north this morning but did not land. Residents of Bhalche were seen on rooftops last night. Residents of Goljung were seen on rooftops last night. Around 11 people are believed to be stranded in Ramche (unconfirmed estimate). Debris is blocking the Bhalche–Goljung road. Debris is blocking the Goljung–Samundratar road. Around 16 people are believed to be stranded in Salme (unconfirmed estimate).
[13:15] Sunita Lama, The Himalayan Times (on the ground): Livestock losses across the district are reported to be severe. Boulders and fallen trees make the Bhalche–Taruka road impassable for any vehicle. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted one injured person (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), one child and five other adults at Ramche, all needing evacuation. Debris has completely choked the road between Dandagaun and Dupcheshwar. The Battar–Samundratar road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. The Goljung–Samundratar road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The Belkot–Bhalche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. Police have checked the Dandagaun–Madanpur road: the social media reports were false and the road is open as normal. Confirmed headcount: Stranded at the temple in Belkot: one injured person (a child with a deep gash on his leg), two children and five other adults. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted four children, three senior citizens and five uninjured adults at the health post in Salme, all needing evacuation. The Bhalche–Likhu road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Confirmed headcount: At Battar, 4 children, three senior citizens and 1 other adult are waiting to be rescued. The only bridge linking Haku and Ramche has collapsed. The Bhalche–Goljung road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Goljung was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Bhalche was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted seven children, 3 senior citizens and two uninjured adults at the school in Dandagaun, all needing evacuation. Our team counted three children and one other adult at the school in Dupcheshwar, all needing evacuation.
[09:30] District Police Office (situation report): Unconfirmed posts on social media claim the Dandagaun–Madanpur road has been washed away. Stranded at the temple in Samundratar: 2 injured people (a child with a deep gash on his leg and a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), two schoolchildren, one senior citizen and one other adult. Residents at Madanpur say one child, one senior citizen and four able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Dupcheshwar–Taruka road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Heavy rain is forecast to continue through the evening.
Anything not mentioned in these dispatches is in its usual condition, and no one else is known to be stranded.
