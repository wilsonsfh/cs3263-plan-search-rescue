FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The Kumari–Taruka road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. The Bhorle–Taruka road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. A landslide has buried the road between Kakani and Taruka under metres of mud. Boulders and fallen trees make the Kumari–Thansing road impassable for any vehicle. Hillside collapse: the Bageshwari–Taruka road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Stranded at the temple in Kakani: one senior citizen and 6 uninjured adults. The Thaprek–Trishuli road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Stranded at Narjamandap: 3 schoolchildren, two senior citizens and four uninjured adults. Stranded at Thansing: three schoolchildren, 3 elderly residents and two uninjured adults. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Bageshwari–Bhorle road; it is open again at normal speed. Residents at the temple in Thaprek say two children, an old man and 2 able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Narjamandap–Taruka road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Residents at the school in Suryagadhi say two injured people (a woman with burns on her arms and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), eight schoolchildren, 3 senior citizens and two uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At Trishuli, one child and two able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. At the school in Bageshwari, one injured person (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), nine schoolchildren, two senior citizens and two able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. At Taruka, three schoolchildren and two uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
