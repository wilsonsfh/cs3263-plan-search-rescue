FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Stranded at Lachyang: 3 injured people (an old man with a head wound, a woman with burns on her arms and a man with a crushed foot), 4 schoolchildren and three other adults. Our team counted four children, two senior citizens and three other adults at Bhalche, all needing evacuation. Tadi was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Our team counted 4 children, one senior citizen and 4 uninjured adults at the ward office in Thansing, all needing evacuation. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Tadi–Taruka road; it is open again at normal speed. The Bidur–Kalikasthan road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Chhap was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. The Bhalche–Bidur road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The Chhap–Taruka road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. The Taruka–Thansing road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. Boulders and fallen trees make the Bhalche–Tadi road impassable for any vehicle. Stranded at the school in Bidur: six children and two able-bodied adults. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
