FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Residents at Battar say 2 schoolchildren, one senior citizen and 5 able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Battar–Suryagadhi road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Residents at the health post in Dandagaun say two children, 2 senior citizens and four able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Sundaradevi was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Kalikasthan–Suryagadhi road; it is open again at normal speed. The Kakani–Tadi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. At Meghang, three children and one other adult are waiting to be rescued. Floodwater has turned the road from Suryagadhi to Tadi into a river. The Battar–Kalikasthan road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Dupcheshwar–Salme road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds a quarter of an hour to the usual journey. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Kalikasthan–Thaprek road; it is open again at normal speed. Hillside collapse: the Gerkhu–Meghang road disappeared under a landslide overnight. At the ward office in Dupcheshwar, two injured people (an old man with a head wound and a woman with burns on her arms), three schoolchildren, an elderly couple and one other adult are waiting to be rescued. At Kakani, three injured people (a woman with burns on her arms, a teenage girl with a fractured arm and a man with a crushed foot), 4 children and four uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Dandagaun–Salme road; it is open again at normal speed. Residents at Tadi say three injured people (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle, an old man with a head wound and a child with a deep gash on his leg), three schoolchildren, two senior citizens and five uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Locals warned us about the Kakani–Suryagadhi road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. The suspension bridge on the Dupcheshwar–Suryagadhi route was swept away by the river. At the temple in Salme, four schoolchildren and 3 able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Stranded at the health post in Gerkhu: one senior citizen and six uninjured adults. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
