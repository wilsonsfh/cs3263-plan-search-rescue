FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The Likhu–Suryagadhi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Stranded at the health post in Bhorle: 3 injured people (a teenage girl with a fractured arm, a young boy with a badly sprained ankle and an old man with a head wound) and five able-bodied adults. Our team counted 6 adults at Jiling, all needing evacuation. The Meghang–Narjamandap road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Debris has completely choked the road between Bhorle and Meghang. The suspension bridge on the Narjamandap–Suryagadhi route was swept away by the river. Residents at Taruka say one injured person (an old man with a head wound), two children and half a dozen other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at the health post in Suryagadhi: one injured person (a woman with burns on her arms), four children, an old man and two able-bodied adults. The Dupcheshwar–Suryagadhi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. Stranded at Thulogaun: three children, two elderly residents and five uninjured adults. The Tadi–Taruka road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Kakani was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. The Battar–Thulogaun road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Our team counted one injured person (a teenage girl with a fractured arm), a baby and 3 able-bodied adults at Likhu, all needing evacuation. Locals warned us about the Chaughada–Kalikasthan road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. Residents at Narjamandap say one schoolchild and two uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Residents at Chhap say two children, an elderly couple and four uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Dupcheshwar–Thaprek road; it is open again at normal speed. The suspension bridge on the Jiling–Kalikasthan route was swept away by the river. At Kalikasthan, three senior citizens and six able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Residents at Gerkhu say 3 children and 4 able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at the school in Betrawati: one injured person (a man with a crushed foot), half a dozen schoolchildren, one senior citizen and two other adults. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Gerkhu–Taruka road; it is open again at normal speed. The only bridge linking Chaughada and Chhap has collapsed. Our team counted two schoolchildren, three elderly residents and three uninjured adults at Dupcheshwar, all needing evacuation. Debris has completely choked the road between Likhu and Thulogaun. At Battar, four children, three senior citizens and 1 uninjured adult are waiting to be rescued. The Chhap–Jiling road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. The Betrawati–Tadi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. At the health post in Meghang, two adults are waiting to be rescued. A landslide has buried the road between Bhorle and Gerkhu under metres of mud. At the ward office in Thaprek, one schoolchild, two senior citizens and six able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Betrawati–Taruka road; it is open again at normal speed. Stranded at Chaughada: four schoolchildren, three senior citizens and six able-bodied adults. Hillside collapse: the Chhap–Samundratar road disappeared under a landslide overnight. The Kakani–Likhu road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
