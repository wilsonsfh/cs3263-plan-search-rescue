FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Stranded at the health post in Bageshwari: one schoolchild, an elderly couple and half a dozen other adults. Our team counted four children, an old man and four able-bodied adults at Haku, all needing evacuation. The Bageshwari–Betrawati road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The Betrawati–Chaughada road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Debris has completely choked the road between Chaughada and Thansing. At the school in Dandagaun, three injured people (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle, a child with a deep gash on his leg and a man with a crushed foot), three schoolchildren, three senior citizens and two uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Debris has completely choked the road between Bidur and Thaprek. Kaule was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Residents at Bhalche say 4 adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At Taruka, one child, three senior citizens and 2 uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Stranded at the health post in Shikharbesi: one injured person (a child with a deep gash on his leg) and six uninjured adults. Our team counted three injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip, a pregnant woman who was hit by debris and a woman with burns on her arms), 2 children, three elderly residents and 3 other adults at the ward office in Panchakanya, all needing evacuation. The Bageshwari–Taruka road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. At the health post in Ramche, one injured person (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), 4 schoolchildren and four uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The Battar–Kaule road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Bidur–Tadi road; it is open again at normal speed. The Haku–Laharepauwa road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. At the school in Chaughada, four schoolchildren and one uninjured adult are waiting to be rescued. The Goljung–Taruka road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Kispang was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. The Dandagaun–Kispang road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. Stranded at the ward office in Battar: two schoolchildren and three uninjured adults. At Thansing, one senior citizen and one able-bodied adult are waiting to be rescued. Stranded at the ward office in Tadi: three schoolchildren, two senior citizens and two able-bodied adults. The Kispang–Ramche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Bageshwari–Laharepauwa road; it is open again at normal speed. Residents at Thaprek say two children, one elderly resident and four other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Our team counted a baby, one senior citizen and three able-bodied adults at Goljung, all needing evacuation. Residents at Laharepauwa say three children, two senior citizens and 6 other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Boulders and fallen trees make the Dandagaun–Ramche road impassable for any vehicle. Everyone from Bidur has already been airlifted out. Locals warned us about the Bhalche–Thansing road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. Floodwater has turned the road from Dupcheshwar to Panchakanya into a river. The Bageshwari–Battar road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. The Bidur–Shikharbesi road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Locals warned us about the Dandagaun–Kaule road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. At Kakani, 3 elderly residents and 1 able-bodied adult are waiting to be rescued. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
