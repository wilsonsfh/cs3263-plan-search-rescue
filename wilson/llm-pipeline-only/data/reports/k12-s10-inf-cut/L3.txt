FIELD DISPATCHES (several sources; listed in the order they reached the control room)
[09:30] District Police Office (situation report): Unconfirmed posts on social media claim the Kispang–Rautbesi road has been washed away. Stranded at Dandagaun: 4 children, 3 elderly residents and 4 other adults. A government helicopter was seen flying north this morning but did not land. At Ghyangphedi, two children, two senior citizens and five uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Stranded at Lachyang: a baby, 3 senior citizens and four able-bodied adults. The Goljung–Syaphru road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. The Belkot–Rautbesi road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. Our team counted one injured person (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), 9 children and two uninjured adults at the school in Syaphru, all needing evacuation. Unconfirmed posts on social media claim the Belkot–Ghyangphedi road has been washed away. The Belkot–Khanigaun road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Unconfirmed posts on social media claim the Haku–Lachyang road has been washed away. Residents at the health post in Likhu say one adult is still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Ramche–Sundaradevi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Unconfirmed posts on social media claim the Haku–Khanigaun road has been washed away. The Okharpauwa–Rautbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Our team counted two injured people (a child with a deep gash on his leg and a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), two senior citizens and three uninjured adults at Khanigaun, all needing evacuation.
