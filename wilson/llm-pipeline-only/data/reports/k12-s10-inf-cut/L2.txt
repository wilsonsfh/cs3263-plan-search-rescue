FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The suspension bridge on the Lachyang–Sundaradevi route was swept away by the river. Locals warned us about the Kispang–Rautbesi road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. The Okharpauwa–Rautbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Haku–Lachyang road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. The Belkot–Khanigaun road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. A landslide has buried the road between Okharpauwa and Syaphru under metres of mud. The Haku–Khanigaun road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. Stranded at Narjamandap: 3 senior citizens and one uninjured adult. The Ramche–Sundaradevi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. At the health post in Likhu, one adult is waiting to be rescued. Stranded at Rautbesi: one child and two able-bodied adults. Our team counted two children, two elderly residents and five able-bodied adults at Ghyangphedi, all needing evacuation. A landslide has buried the road between Belkot and Ramche under metres of mud. Stranded at Goljung: 3 elderly residents and four able-bodied adults. Our team counted one injured person (a man with a crushed foot), nine children and 2 other adults at the school in Syaphru, all needing evacuation. The Goljung–Syaphru road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. Our team counted a baby, three elderly residents and half a dozen uninjured adults at Sundaradevi, all needing evacuation. Stranded at Khanigaun: two injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), two elderly residents and three uninjured adults. A landslide has buried the road between Dandagaun and Goljung under metres of mud. Residents at Dandagaun say four children, three senior citizens and four uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Belkot–Ghyangphedi road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. At Lachyang, one schoolchild, three senior citizens and four able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Goljung–Okharpauwa road; it is open again at normal speed. Everyone from Ramche has already been airlifted out. Floodwater has turned the road from Belkot to Rautbesi into a river. Boulders and fallen trees make the Ghyangphedi–Kispang road impassable for any vehicle. Our team counted a baby, one senior citizen and two uninjured adults at Haku, all needing evacuation. At the school in Kispang, three injured people (a woman with burns on her arms, an old man with a head wound and a child with a deep gash on his leg), three children, two senior citizens and two uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
