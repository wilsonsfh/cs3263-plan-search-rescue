FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
A landslide has buried the road between Kalikasthan and Kispang under metres of mud. Residents at the school in Kakani say four schoolchildren and two other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Our team counted four schoolchildren, two elderly residents and four other adults at Laharepauwa, all needing evacuation. Floodwater has turned the road from Kispang to Sundaradevi into a river. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Salme–Thansing road; it is open again at normal speed. Stranded at Salme: three adults. Stranded at the health post in Sundaradevi: four children and two able-bodied adults. The Battar–Sundaradevi road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Our team counted six schoolchildren and 1 able-bodied adult at the school in Haku, all needing evacuation. Everyone from Suryagadhi has already been airlifted out. The Kispang–Suryagadhi road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The Kakani–Kalikasthan road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Kakani–Kispang road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Stranded at the school in Thansing: one injured person (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), 9 schoolchildren and two uninjured adults. The Haku–Kalikasthan road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Our team counted two schoolchildren and 1 able-bodied adult at the ward office in Kalikasthan, all needing evacuation. Stranded at the temple in Battar: one injured person (an elderly woman with a broken hip), one schoolchild and 6 uninjured adults. The Laharepauwa–Meghang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The Haku–Sundaradevi road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Kispang–Meghang road; it is open again at normal speed. Hillside collapse: the Meghang–Salme road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
