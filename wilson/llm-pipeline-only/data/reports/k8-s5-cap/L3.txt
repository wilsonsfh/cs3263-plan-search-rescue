Confirmed headcount: Stranded at the health post in Sundaradevi: four schoolchildren and two uninjured adults. Stranded at the school in Thansing: one injured person (a man with a crushed foot), 9 children and two other adults. Confirmed headcount: At the school in Kakani, four children and 2 able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Everyone from Suryagadhi has already been airlifted out. Mobile networks remain patchy across the district. The Kakani–Kalikasthan road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Confirmed headcount: At Laharepauwa, 4 children, two senior citizens and four uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The Kispang–Meghang road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. [09:30] District Police Office (situation report): At the school in Haku, 6 schoolchildren and one able-bodied adult are waiting to be rescued. Heavy rain is forecast to continue through the evening. Unconfirmed posts on social media claim the Haku–Sundaradevi road has been washed away. Residents at the ward office in Kalikasthan say two schoolchildren and one able-bodied adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At Salme, three adults are waiting to be rescued. A landslide has buried the road between Meghang and Salme under metres of mud. At the temple in Battar, one injured person (a teenage girl with a fractured arm), one schoolchild and six other adults are waiting to be rescued. The Haku–Kalikasthan road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Kispang–Sundaradevi road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater.
