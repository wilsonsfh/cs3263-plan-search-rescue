FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The Bageshwari–Haku road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 15 minutes to the usual journey. At Battar, four schoolchildren, three elderly residents and five uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The suspension bridge on the Bageshwari–Ghyangphedi route was swept away by the river. Debris has completely choked the road between Betrawati and Madanpur. The Bhalche–Chaughada road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The Kalikasthan–Meghang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Stranded at Chaughada: a baby and 5 other adults. The only bridge linking Battar and Bhalche has collapsed. Belkot was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Residents at Ghyangphedi say four children and six uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Stranded at Kalikasthan: one injured person (a child with a deep gash on his leg), four children and one uninjured adult. Stranded at the health post in Sundaradevi: one schoolchild and 3 other adults. At the health post in Bageshwari, an elderly couple and 2 other adults are waiting to be rescued. The Kalikasthan–Trishuli road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. At Tadi, two children, an old man and 2 uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. Floodwater has turned the road from Chaughada to Madanpur into a river. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Bageshwari–Sundaradevi road; it is open again at normal speed. Our team counted three children and one other adult at Haku, all needing evacuation. At the school in Trishuli, six schoolchildren, two senior citizens and one able-bodied adult are waiting to be rescued. Bhalche was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. At Meghang, two children, one senior citizen and two uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. At Thaprek, two injured people (an old man with a head wound and a man with a crushed foot), a baby, three senior citizens and three other adults are waiting to be rescued. The only bridge linking Chaughada and Thaprek has collapsed. Our team counted three schoolchildren and five other adults at Madanpur, all needing evacuation. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
