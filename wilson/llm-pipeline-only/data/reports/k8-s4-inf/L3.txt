FIELD DISPATCHES (several sources; listed in the order they reached the control room)
[09:30] District Police Office (situation report): The Goljung–Okharpauwa road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The only bridge linking Lachyang and Meghang has collapsed. Heavy rain is forecast to continue through the evening.
[13:15] Sunita Lama, The Himalayan Times (on the ground): Confirmed headcount: At the health post in Okharpauwa, one child, two senior citizens and three able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted a baby and 5 other adults at Lachyang, all needing evacuation. The Meghang–Taruka road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted three injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip, a teenage girl with a fractured arm and a child with a deep gash on his leg), 5 schoolchildren and 2 other adults at the school in Chhap, all needing evacuation. Boulders and fallen trees make the Okharpauwa–Taruka road impassable for any vehicle, cutting a road that was open earlier today. Our team counted eight schoolchildren, one elderly resident and two able-bodied adults at the school in Tupche, all needing evacuation. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted 4 children and half a dozen other adults at Shikharbesi, all needing evacuation. Confirmed headcount: Residents at Goljung say two injured people (a woman with burns on her arms and a man with a crushed foot), one schoolchild, an old man and four able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Floodwater has turned the road from Chhap to Tupche into a river. The Goljung–Lachyang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Confirmed headcount: Stranded at Chaughada: one injured person (a man with a crushed foot), one child and 4 able-bodied adults. Livestock losses across the district are reported to be severe. Betrawati was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Chaughada–Lachyang road; it is open again at normal speed. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted four adults at the ward office in Taruka, all needing evacuation.
[06:45] Radio Nuwakot FM (local radio, early bulletin): Around 4 people are believed to be stranded in Chaughada (unconfirmed estimate). Debris is blocking the Chaughada–Lachyang road. Around 7 people are believed to be stranded in Taruka (unconfirmed estimate). Around 5 people are believed to be stranded in Goljung (unconfirmed estimate). Around 7 people are believed to be stranded in Chhap (unconfirmed estimate). Mobile networks remain patchy across the district. Around 10 people are believed to be stranded in Okharpauwa (unconfirmed estimate). Residents of Betrawati were seen on rooftops last night. The Okharpauwa–Taruka road is still passable this morning, drivers say. Around 10 people are believed to be stranded in Lachyang (unconfirmed estimate). Around 13 people are believed to be stranded in Shikharbesi (unconfirmed estimate).
