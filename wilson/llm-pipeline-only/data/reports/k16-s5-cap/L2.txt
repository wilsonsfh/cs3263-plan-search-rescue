FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Boulders and fallen trees make the Belkot–Chaughada road impassable for any vehicle. Stranded at the ward office in Kalikasthan: four children, an old man and four uninjured adults. Residents at Bidur say one elderly resident and six uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Kumari was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Our team counted 2 schoolchildren, three senior citizens and 6 able-bodied adults at Kaule, all needing evacuation. Hillside collapse: the Belkot–Likhu road disappeared under a landslide overnight. The Ghyangphedi–Madanpur road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Residents at the ward office in Sundaradevi say four schoolchildren, an elderly couple and one other adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At Goljung, three children, an elderly couple and three other adults are waiting to be rescued. The Bhalche–Thulogaun road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Everyone from Salme has already been airlifted out. Hillside collapse: the Meghang–Ramche road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Residents at Madanpur say two children, 3 elderly residents and three uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Bhorle–Bidur road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Residents at Bhorle say one injured person (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), four children, two elderly residents and two uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Residents at Ramche say three elderly residents and four able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Locals warned us about the Bidur–Madanpur road, yet it is intact and passable at normal speed. The Chhap–Kalikasthan road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The Haku–Meghang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Our team counted two injured people (an old man with a head wound and a teenage girl with a fractured arm) and three able-bodied adults at Meghang, all needing evacuation. Hillside collapse: the Ghyangphedi–Kumari road disappeared under a landslide overnight. The Goljung–Salme road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. At Thulogaun, 2 children, three elderly residents and 3 other adults are waiting to be rescued. The Likhu–Thansing road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. The Belkot–Kumari road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The Goljung–Panchakanya road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds a quarter of an hour to the usual journey. The Bhalche–Syaphru road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Thansing–Thulogaun road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Residents at the health post in Chaughada say three injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris, a child with a deep gash on his leg and a woman with burns on her arms) and three uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At the ward office in Haku, 2 children, two senior citizens and three able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Debris has completely choked the road between Bidur and Chaughada. Residents at the temple in Chhap say two children and four able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Our team counted 4 adults at the ward office in Ghyangphedi, all needing evacuation. Floodwater has turned the road from Goljung to Laharepauwa into a river. Our team counted four children, one elderly resident and three other adults at Thansing, all needing evacuation. Residents at the temple in Laharepauwa say two schoolchildren, an old man and one able-bodied adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
