FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
The Dupcheshwar–Meghang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Our team counted two injured people (an old man with a head wound and a woman with burns on her arms), four children, one elderly resident and five other adults at Kispang, all needing evacuation. Stranded at the school in Suryagadhi: 5 children and 1 uninjured adult. Our team counted two injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip and a woman with burns on her arms), four children, three elderly residents and four able-bodied adults at the health post in Trishuli, all needing evacuation. The Rautbesi–Trishuli road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Boulders and fallen trees make the Narjamandap–Suryagadhi road impassable for any vehicle. The Narjamandap–Rautbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. Hillside collapse: the Narjamandap–Panchakanya road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Panchakanya was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Stranded at the temple in Meghang: three injured people (a child with a deep gash on his leg, a pregnant woman who was hit by debris and a young boy with a badly sprained ankle), a baby and three uninjured adults. The Kispang–Panchakanya road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Everyone from Rautbesi has already been airlifted out. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
