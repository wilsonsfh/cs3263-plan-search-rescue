FIELD DISPATCHES (several sources; listed in the order they reached the control room)
[06:45] Radio Nuwakot FM (local radio, early bulletin): Debris is blocking the Kispang–Panchakanya road. Livestock losses across the district are reported to be severe. Residents of Rautbesi were seen on rooftops last night. Debris is blocking the Rautbesi–Trishuli road. Residents of Panchakanya were seen on rooftops last night. Around 10 people are believed to be stranded in Trishuli (unconfirmed estimate). The Narjamandap–Suryagadhi road is still passable this morning, drivers say.
[09:30] District Police Office (situation report): Residents at the school in Suryagadhi say 5 children and one able-bodied adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Dupcheshwar–Meghang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. At Kispang, two injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris and a teenage girl with a fractured arm), four children, an old man and five uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. In Kathmandu, schools have been closed for three days as a precaution. Residents at the temple in Meghang say 3 injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip, a man with a crushed foot and a child with a deep gash on his leg), one schoolchild and three uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors.
[13:15] Sunita Lama, The Himalayan Times (on the ground): Everyone from Panchakanya has already been airlifted out. The Home Ministry says relief funds will be released next week. Everyone from Rautbesi has already been airlifted out. Hillside collapse: the Narjamandap–Panchakanya road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Kispang–Panchakanya road; it is open again at normal speed. Debris has completely choked the road between Narjamandap and Suryagadhi, cutting a road that was open earlier today. The Narjamandap–Rautbesi road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Rautbesi–Trishuli road; it is open again at normal speed. Confirmed headcount: Residents at the health post in Trishuli say 2 injured people (an elderly woman with a broken hip and a woman with burns on her arms), four schoolchildren, 3 senior citizens and 4 able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors.
Anything not mentioned in these dispatches is in its usual condition, and no one else is known to be stranded.
