FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Stranded at the school in Kispang: two injured people (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle and a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), four children, 3 elderly residents and two other adults. Our team counted two children, three elderly residents and 2 other adults at the ward office in Ramche, all needing evacuation. The Likhu–Ramche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Hillside collapse: the Kalikasthan–Ramche road disappeared under a landslide overnight. At the ward office in Kalikasthan, four children, one senior citizen and five uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The Kispang–Likhu road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The Dupcheshwar–Kispang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. The only bridge linking Dupcheshwar and Likhu has collapsed. Debris has completely choked the road between Dupcheshwar and Kalikasthan. Stranded at Taruka: two injured people (an old man with a head wound and a man with a crushed foot), an old man and 2 uninjured adults. The suspension bridge on the Dupcheshwar–Taruka route was swept away by the river. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
