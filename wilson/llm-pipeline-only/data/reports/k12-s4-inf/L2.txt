FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
Our team counted four schoolchildren, an elderly couple and five able-bodied adults at Kaule, all needing evacuation. The Battar–Panchakanya road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Laharepauwa was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. Our team counted five children, three elderly residents and one other adult at the school in Taruka, all needing evacuation. Our team counted 2 elderly residents and 2 other adults at the health post in Chaughada, all needing evacuation. Residents at the health post in Kispang say two senior citizens and four able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At the health post in Dhaibung, an old man and five able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. The Battar–Dandagaun road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Dhaibung–Dupcheshwar road; it is open again at normal speed. The Laharepauwa–Ramche road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Dandagaun–Tadi road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. The suspension bridge on the Laharepauwa–Salme route was swept away by the river. Residents at Dandagaun say 3 schoolchildren and three able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Betrawati–Kaule road; it is open again at normal speed. Residents at Betrawati say one elderly resident and four other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Likhu was fully evacuated by the army yesterday; no one remains there. The Ramche–Salme road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Kaule–Likhu road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Floodwater has turned the road from Laharepauwa to Tadi into a river. At the health post in Khanigaun, three injured people (an old man with a head wound, a woman with burns on her arms and a child with a deep gash on his leg), four schoolchildren, one elderly resident and three able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. At Ramche, a baby, an old man and one other adult are waiting to be rescued. At Panchakanya, two adults are waiting to be rescued. The Dhaibung–Taruka road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Hillside collapse: the Dupcheshwar–Taruka road disappeared under a landslide overnight. The Gerkhu–Likhu road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. Our team counted two injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris and a woman with burns on her arms), 8 children, two senior citizens and one able-bodied adult at the school in Battar, all needing evacuation. Everyone from Dupcheshwar has already been airlifted out. At Gerkhu, two injured people (a young boy with a badly sprained ankle and a woman with burns on her arms), one schoolchild, an old man and half a dozen able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Hillside collapse: the Khanigaun–Laharepauwa road disappeared under a landslide overnight. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
