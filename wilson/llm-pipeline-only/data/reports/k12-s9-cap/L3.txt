[09:30] District Police Office (situation report): The only bridge linking Betrawati and Thansing has collapsed. Debris has completely choked the road between Kumari and Panchakanya. The Battar–Betrawati road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The suspension bridge on the Narjamandap–Salme route was swept away by the river. The Battar–Panchakanya road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. The Bageshwari–Narjamandap road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Stranded at Bageshwari: one injured person (a woman with burns on her arms), 2 elderly residents and three able-bodied adults. Livestock losses across the district are reported to be severe.
