[13:15] Sunita Lama, The Himalayan Times (on the ground): Residents at Gerkhu say 4 adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Confirmed headcount: Our team counted two children, three senior citizens and six other adults at Betrawati, all needing evacuation. The Belkot–Chaughada road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 20 minutes to the usual journey. The Bhalche–Thulogaun road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. At Chaughada, two adults are waiting to be rescued. The Home Ministry says relief funds will be released next week. Our team counted 3 schoolchildren, an old man and 2 other adults at the school in Tadi, all needing evacuation. The Bhorle–Rautbesi road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Goljung–Sundaradevi road; it is open again at normal speed. Confirmed headcount: At Bhorle, two children, one senior citizen and one other adult are waiting to be rescued. [09:30] District Police Office (situation report): Residents at Thulogaun say three injured people (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris, an old man with a head wound and a man with a crushed foot), three children and six able-bodied adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Likhu–Meghang road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Stranded at the health post in Meghang: one injured person (a pregnant woman who was hit by debris), three senior citizens and three able-bodied adults.
