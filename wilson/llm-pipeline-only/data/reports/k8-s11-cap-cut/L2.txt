FIELD DISPATCH (Prakash Thapa, Kantipur correspondent, filed 12:10)
At Taruka, four schoolchildren, one senior citizen and one uninjured adult are waiting to be rescued. Residents at Syaphru say 2 injured people (a child with a deep gash on his leg and an old man with a head wound), two schoolchildren and three other adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. Hillside collapse: the Bageshwari–Chhap road disappeared under a landslide overnight. The only bridge linking Kalikasthan and Suryagadhi has collapsed. Army engineers have cleared the debris on the Samundratar–Suryagadhi road; it is open again at normal speed. Stranded at Tupche: two children and four other adults. At Betrawati, two children, one senior citizen and 3 able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. Debris has completely choked the road between Dhaibung and Taruka. The Chhap–Dandagaun road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. The Dandagaun–Tupche road is muddy and frightening to drive, but our jeep got through in the usual time. The Bageshwari–Dandagaun road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it now takes double the normal journey time. The Kalikasthan–Samundratar road is under two metres of fast-flowing floodwater. A landslide has buried the road between Betrawati and Taruka under metres of mud. Our team counted four children, an old man and 5 uninjured adults at Suryagadhi, all needing evacuation. Stranded at Dandagaun: three schoolchildren and four uninjured adults. Residents at the health post in Samundratar say one injured person (a woman with burns on her arms), two children and 1 able-bodied adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. At the temple in Kalikasthan, two schoolchildren and five able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. The Chhap–Dhaibung road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. The Dhaibung–Kalikasthan road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds a quarter of an hour to the usual journey. Apart from what is described here, roads are in their usual condition and no one else is known to be stranded.
