FIELD DISPATCHES (several sources; listed in the order they reached the control room)
[13:15] Sunita Lama, The Himalayan Times (on the ground): At Betrawati, 2 schoolchildren, an old man and three other adults are waiting to be rescued. Floodwater has turned the road from Kalikasthan to Samundratar into a river, cutting a road that was open earlier today. Confirmed headcount: At Syaphru, two injured people (a teenage girl with a fractured arm and an elderly woman with a broken hip), two schoolchildren and 3 uninjured adults are waiting to be rescued. The Bageshwari–Dandagaun road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it takes twice the usual time. Police have checked the Dandagaun–Tupche road: the social media reports were false and the road is open as normal. Confirmed headcount: At the health post in Samundratar, one injured person (a woman with burns on her arms), two schoolchildren and 1 other adult are waiting to be rescued. In Kathmandu, schools have been closed for three days as a precaution. The Dhaibung–Kalikasthan road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds a quarter of an hour to the usual journey. Hillside collapse: the Betrawati–Taruka road disappeared under a landslide overnight, cutting a road that was open earlier today. Confirmed headcount: Residents at the temple in Kalikasthan say 2 children and five uninjured adults are still there, sheltering on upper floors. A landslide has buried the road between Bageshwari and Chhap under metres of mud. Confirmed headcount: Residents at Taruka say four children, an old man and one uninjured adult are still there, sheltering on upper floors. The Samundratar–Suryagadhi road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal. Confirmed headcount: At Suryagadhi, 4 children, one elderly resident and five other adults are waiting to be rescued. Confirmed headcount: At Dandagaun, three children and four able-bodied adults are waiting to be rescued. The only bridge linking Kalikasthan and Suryagadhi has collapsed. Stranded at Tupche: two schoolchildren and four other adults. The Chhap–Dhaibung road is open but badly waterlogged; driving it adds 10 minutes to the usual journey. The Chhap–Dandagaun road has been reopened after bulldozers removed the debris; travel times are back to normal.
[06:45] Radio Nuwakot FM (local radio, early bulletin): Debris is blocking the Chhap–Dandagaun road. A government helicopter was seen flying north this morning but did not land. Around 11 people are believed to be stranded in Syaphru (unconfirmed estimate). The Betrawati–Taruka road is still passable this morning, drivers say. Around 9 people are believed to be stranded in Taruka (unconfirmed estimate). Around 8 people are believed to be stranded in Suryagadhi (unconfirmed estimate). Around 1 people are believed to be stranded in Samundratar (unconfirmed estimate). Around 4 people are believed to be stranded in Kalikasthan (unconfirmed estimate). The Kalikasthan–Samundratar road is still passable this morning, drivers say. Debris is blocking the Samundratar–Suryagadhi road. Around 4 people are believed to be stranded in Dandagaun (unconfirmed estimate).
[09:30] District Police Office (situation report): Heavy rain is forecast to continue through the evening. Unconfirmed posts on social media claim the Dandagaun–Tupche road has been washed away. Boulders and fallen trees make the Dhaibung–Taruka road impassable for any vehicle.
Anything not mentioned in these dispatches is in its usual condition, and no one else is known to be stranded.
